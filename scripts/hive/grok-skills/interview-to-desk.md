# Interview-to-desk (funnel)

**Stack:** Grok Big Boss intake. Consultant/Researcher on the triangle. Cursor only after a slice is named.

## When
“Interview me and propose the desk.” New business lane. Portfolio shift.

## Steps
1. Interview: clog, leak, done-definition, constraints. Ask until sure.
2. **Founders triangle** (one green = go; zero = don’t):
   - domain (years of intuition)
   - depth (can you go deep)
   - distribution (can you reach buyers)
3. Pick **one TEAM letter** this week: T ops · E product · A admin · M marketing.
4. Pick **one recurring task** in that letter — not the whole machine.
5. Map to existing 17 agents. Do not create an 18th.
6. Write context docs the Gmail thread will never capture (OPERATOR_MEMORY / client packet).
7. Watcher = Grok Watchdog (friction log). Not a new “Toby.”

## Stop
`business-lanes.json` change and any build burn need operator yes.

## Anti-patterns
- Hundreds of agents
- Skipping the triangle
- Building before one ICP sentence (`outcome-offer-funnel`)
