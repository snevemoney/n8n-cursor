After `discovery-spiced-constraint` on a live or scheduled constraint call. They asked to see the leak/fix. Not a cold demo blast.

## Steps
1. **Constraint (ops sigh)** — their words. Numbers = owner only; else UNVERIFIED.
2. **Speed-to-lead ~90s** — Loom / screen-share until public Vercel. Never paste `127.0.0.1` or `:3007` in a client note.
3. **Cinematic ~30s** — public URL only (cinematic demo when deployed). Skip if the ICP is trade-callback, not premium page.
4. **MCP** — only if they are technical. Public connector demo. Not STL.
5. **XOR ask** — Rung 0 diagnosis **or** Rung 1 intake→book. One ask. CAD band from Money Desk, not tape $.
6. Draft the recap via `warm-draft-hitl`. Dual gate. Evens sends.
7. If they book a callback window: draft the slot in `private-book-install`. Do not fire Calendly.
