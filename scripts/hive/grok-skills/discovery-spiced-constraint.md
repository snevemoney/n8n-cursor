# Discovery — SPICED + constraint

**Stack:** Cursor + Grok Bot. Consultant assists. Evens is on the call.

## When
They replied, objected, asked for a deck/price, or a constraint call is scheduled. After `warm-draft-hitl` (sent by Evens). Before SKU freeze. Not another cold blast (`outbound-playbook-funnel` step 8).

## Steps
1. **Situation** — busy week; tools they already pay for.
2. **Pain** — where they *sigh* (inbox, no-shows, DMs, broken apply). Their words only.
3. **Impact** — what slips. Numbers = owner words; else UNVERIFIED / TBD.
4. **Critical event** — hiring, launch, capacity crunch, the week that made it acute.
5. **Decision** — who says yes (#4/#16); timeline ≤60d?
6. **Sigh test** — slow SLA vs intentional scarcity. Intentional → Rung 0 diagnosis, not speed install.
7. Close with a **reversible** next: 20-min recap / private book test / audit kit. No custom app language.
8. Outputs: notes → update `four-blank-sku` (one numbered KPI) → CONFIRM path or WALK.
9. Price: packet band only. Do not invent job $. Then `demo-walk-script` if they want to see it.

## Stop
Send / pay / book = operator. Discovery is notes + blanks, not a close.
