# Outbound playbook funnel

**Stack:** Grok writes the playbook. Cursor holds list files. Communications drafts. HITL sends.

## When
A **shortlist** is ready and Path A is green through margin (or operator waived).  
After `list-anneal-funnel`, only the 3–5 that passed MUST — not the raw 50.  
Named client already scored → skip anneal; start here after `warm-draft-hitl` is allowed.

## Steps
1. Ingest: offer, ICP, objections, case studies → training modules.
2. **Certification gate:** humans are not approved to send until the playbook exists.
3. Playbook: intro, pitch, downsell, qualify, top 5–7 objections, question bank.
4. Personalization triggers on the account so the note can branch.
5. **Fast between touches** (CRM, draft, disposition). **Slow with the prospect.**
6. Same-day QA on every send/call — notes that day, not 500 later.
7. Draft via `warm-draft-hitl`. Dual gate: approve draft ≠ approve send.
8. After they reply: `discovery-spiced-constraint` then `demo-walk-script`. Not another cold blast.

## Stop
Send / dial / book = operator. No auto-dialer. No mass-DM.

## Anti-patterns
- AI on the call instead of around the call
- Course/community CTA as the product
- Farms or fake-follower distribution
