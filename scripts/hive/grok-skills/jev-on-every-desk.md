# Jev on every desk

Typed decisions are allowed on every desk. Follow the numbered steps in `CONTENT/topics/jev-on-every-desk-20260921.md` under "Use this". Short form:

1. Chat writes. Jev decides. Code acts.
2. Menu is text already on the page, plus `none` / `no tool` / `more than one tool`.
3. Filter first. Side talk stays off the menu.
4. Choice, then a separate yes/no: does an answer exist? If no, do not take the top choice.
5. One action, then look again. Three no-change actions = blocked.
6. Send, pay, deploy, book, publish are never menu items.
7. Jev cannot see video. It picks a timestamp only after transcription.
8. Do not point it at reach or money.
9. Their 0.85, 7.1s, and 193× are not our numbers. Our card is still EVALUATED.
10. A string model does not make the staked call. Name the typed task first. Stakes stay with Evens. Do not email TypeSafe or open careers.
11. Show the decision, the wait, and the cost beside the choice. Do not build a particle face. Do not copy a demo’s dollar figure.
12. Router first, gate last. The expensive model is not asked if the job is simple. Their “zero approvals” is not our rule. Hard steps still ask Evens.
13. Shadow first. Decide inside a closed set (state, choice, score, yes/no). Code executes, then a real check verifies. Low confidence goes to review. A released workflow can still have an unverified business result.
14. One call can hold a choice, a score, and a probability. You name the options. It cannot write the sentence. Their 200× and “zero hallucinations” stay theirs.
15. Several yes-probabilities on one phrase. Act only if they combine. Otherwise the line is heard and not handled. A demo’s percents are not our threshold.
16. The deck, or the focused app’s controls named in text, is the menu. Jev picks one. It does not write the slide or the sentence. Trader, autopilot, and ad-scan claims are refused.
17. A transcript is a separate job. Jev picks a timestamp already in it, then a closed score. A person reviews. Code cuts.
18. If the same failure is still the state, stop and do not retry. `2101433560796467348`
19. If the target is missing or the utterance is unfinished, ask a person and do not act. `2100770533633605876`
20. If the confidence has not been tested on labeled examples, ask a person and do not treat the label as proof. `2099925682726002904`
21. Rebuild the menu every turn from what exists now, because a stale menu is yesterday’s actions. `2101717533346918478`
22. Compaction is keep-or-drop, not a summary. `2101705843200721203`
23. Writing text, choosing from a closed list, and code checking the outcome are three different jobs. `2101717533346918478`
24. Dry-run first. `2101811009455358121`
25. Kill switch stays. It is `enabled: false` or bypass. `2101811009455358121`
26. Hide the view counts when scoring hook strength. `2102042713160028244`

**Accurate.** Confidence is not accuracy. Pair the pick with a known-answer check. Low confidence stops and asks Evens.

**Fast.** Filter first. One request, many questions. Rebuild the menu when the page changes.

**Cheap.** OpenRouter `typesafe/jev-1.13` ($0.042 / $0 per 1M). Do not spend a chat model on a yes/no. Talk stays the chat model.

**Allowed.** Every desk, inside its lane.

**Not allowed.** Send, pay, deploy, book, publish. Those stay Evens. No TypeSafe SDK. No jev-ultrafast install. No 193× as our number.
