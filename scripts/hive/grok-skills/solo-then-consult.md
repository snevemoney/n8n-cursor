# Solo then consult

**Stack:** Cursor + Grok Bot. Nate School / Plus community = on tape only.

## Loop

one ICP + one leak → MUST + margin → Loom before/after → HITL send → deliver → capture metrics → then raise to consultant framing (design the system, not “tell me what to build”)

## Steps

1. **Do not open an agency.** 17 named agents are the bench. No nameless delivery hire until 3–5 paid Path A proofs.
2. **One person, one pain.** Not “I automate anything.” Pick `icp_id` from the steal sheet.
3. **Money spine first.** MUST → constraint → four-blank → margin. Under-scoped $1,500 builds are the trap on tape.
4. **Show the destination.** 2-min Loom / `demo-walk-script`. AI is the car; booked callback / hours saved is the destination.
5. **Speak time and money only from owner numbers.** Do not invent hours × $100. Pain $ stays UNVERIFIED until they say it.
6. **Consultant pitch after proof.** “Let me figure out what you need” = POSITION. Not “are you excited about AI?” — leak gates, not AI-bullishness. Normand is a leak.
7. **Metrics after live.** `proof-30-60-90`. Then retainers are optional, HITL, not the first ask.

## Never

Agency-first · free custom build as default · Accenture / $7K–$4M / $600M as our price · hours×rate fanfic · AI-excitement as a MUST score · School community as a lane · rotate hunt off Normand · new `icp_id` from this video.
