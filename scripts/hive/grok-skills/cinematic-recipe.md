3. Creative clip: stills → Seedance/Runway → grade. For the full grade list load `motion-grade-pipeline`.

5. Fail-the-build pass. CTA visible **before scroll**, repeated after proof, unique color (`gt8k4bA01Mo`). Then `click-live-site` + `verify-after-browser`. “Looks good” is a fail.
