# Lead web find

**Stack:** Cursor + Grok Bot. Lead Hunter finds. Researcher scores. No outreach.

## When
- One named URL, or “find a few leaky sites.”
- After `list-anneal-funnel` — only the **3–5** shortlist, not the raw 50.

**Use `list-anneal-funnel` instead** when the ask is volume / “pull 50.”

## Steps
1. Tag `icp_id` from `CONTENT/icp-runbooks/INDEX.md` (clinic ≠ plumber ≠ restaurant ≠ law ≠ fitness). Do not invent an ICP.
2. Find **owner-operator** sites (not enterprise committees).
3. Capture **public leakage**: 404 apply, mailto:null, email-only, no book, newsletter≠intake, after-hours dead end.
4. One-sentence constraint hypothesis (ACQUIRE or CUT). Mark hypothesis.
5. Usable contact (form, email, book, LinkedIn). None → skip.
6. Hand Researcher: URL · contact · constraint · `icp_id` · city (default Greater Montreal).
7. `prospect-must-score` then Path A in `website-offer-funnel`.
8. Append `HUNT_LOG.md` with **stage** (`discovered` minimum). Real URL — `url=—` is dropped by `hunt-log-stats.py`.
9. Stop. Do not draft warm notes or score MUSTs yourself.

## Stop
Send / pay / book = operator. This skill does not outreach.

## Never
MUST-score the raw 50 · CE lead write · cold DM that implies send · PHI/PCI targets for a micro-fee · always-plumber tag · OTP / IG farms · auto-dial · Claude Cowork/Code, Codex, ChatGPT, Gemini, Coda, Vapi, Abacus.
