# Proof 30 / 60 / 90

**Stack:** Cursor + Grok Bot. Money Desk + Consultant. Not a catalog upgrade by itself.

## When
Post-install for a **paid** client (invoice/deposit started). Not for unpaid warm asks. After `private-book-install` is live on *their* site.

## Inputs
- Four blanks (Bucket, one numbered KPI, Baseline, 60-day)
- Owner system of record (calendar, form, inbox). Their counts only.

## Steps
1. **~30d** — is the rail live? (book path, alerts, CTA). Binary yes/no + blockers. No volume invention.
2. **~60d** — KPI vs baseline. Owner-provided counts only. Gaps = UNKNOWN / UNVERIFIED.
3. **~90d** — recommend **keep retainer ($500–1K/mo early)** / **upsell Rung 2** / **stop**.
4. Money Desk: delivery hours vs fee. If margin broke, shrink next time — do not raise fake ROI.
5. Short memo to Evens + GTM. HITL before any client-facing claim.
6. Catalog upgrade (`catalog-lane-upgrade.py`) only after pilot PASS + `--operator-yes`. This skill does not upgrade.

## Stop
Pay / publish / upgrade / retainer ask = operator. Proof memo is internal until Evens says send.

## Never
Invent inquiry/close rates · quote educator case studies as this client · push retainer before 30d rail-live · `--operator-yes` from Cursor · Claude Cowork/Code, Codex, ChatGPT, Gemini, Coda, Vapi, Abacus · operate farms.
