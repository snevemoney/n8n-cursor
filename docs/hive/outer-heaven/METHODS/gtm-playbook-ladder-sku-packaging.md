## 2) SKU packaging rules (GTM owns)

1. **Four blanks required:** Bucket · KPI · Baseline · 60-day target (Baseline may be TBD).  
2. **Bucket first:** ACQUIRE / CUT (GROW later — don’t sell first).  
3. **Shrink scope, not price** when margin threatened.  
4. **Pain-% narrative** only with verified volume; else fixed band.  
5. **Private apply→book** for premium brands; loud “Book free” only when brand fits.  
6. **Diagnosis before install** when SLA might be intentional (Hoyt pattern).  
7. **Demos are proofs, not delivery stack:**  
   - https://cinematic-ai-partner.vercel.app  
   - https://mcp-connector-demo.vercel.app  
   - Speed-to-Lead: public when live (never paste localhost into client-facing drafts)  
8. **Client delivery PASS:** Cal.com/Calendly · Typeform/Tally · WP/Squarespace CTA · email/SMS alert · private book · reminders · Loom  
9. **Client delivery FAIL first ask:** custom Next app · CE/hive · full redesign · ads · autonomous send
