## Ladder (CAD) — SoT for first deals

| Rung | Name | Band | Default delivery | When to sell | When to walk |
|------|------|------|------------------|--------------|--------------|
| 0 | Enablement | $750–1.2K | Private calendar behind approve, CTA fix, notify, Loom | Premium brand / unsure if speed wanted (Hoyt-class) | Ads/chatbot closer |

**Retainer SoT (Money Desk):** early operator reality = **$500–1K/mo**. Playbook/educator **$3–10K/mo** = stretch / UNVERIFIED — do not quote as ours until volume.

## Prototype delivery (≤40%)
PASS: Cal.com/Calendly, Typeform/Tally, WP/Squarespace CTA, email/SMS alert, private book, reminders, Loom  
FAIL first ask: custom Next app as deliverable, CE/hive install, full redesign, ads, autonomous send  
Demos (cinematic / MCP / STL) = **sales proofs only**, not client delivery stack.

## Scoring use cases
1. Broken book/apply → Rung 1 strong  
2. Working slow premium SLA → Rung 0 until pain  
3. Newsletter ≠ book → Rung 0–1  
4. DM intake no CRM → Rung 1 if HITL OK  
5. No-shows/reminders → Rung 0–1 add-on  
6. Firm HR templates → not default micro-install  
7. No contact → DQ  
8. Committee for <$3.5K → DQ
