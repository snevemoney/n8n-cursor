If you're starting a business **by yourself**, especially an online/AI/business venture that you personally own, select **Business – Sole Proprietorship** on that CRA screen.

A **trust is not the "empire" version of a business**. 😄 Legally, it's a very different structure: a trustee holds/administers property or assets for beneficiaries. Québec also specifically treats a commercial trust as its own legal form with different tax and registration obligations. citeturn0search6turn0search11

For what you're doing, think of the progression more like:

**You → Sole proprietorship → Corporation → potentially holding company / trust structures later**

A sole proprietorship is the simplest starting structure: you're the owner, you receive the profits, and the business income is reported through your personal tax return. The trade-off is that you and the business are **not separate legal entities**, so you personally assume the business's risks/liabilities. citeturn0search4turn0search12

If the business eventually becomes substantial—meaning meaningful profits, employees, valuable IP/software, investments, multiple businesses, etc.—then **incorporation** becomes the structure I'd evaluate, rather than changing from sole proprietorship to a trust.
