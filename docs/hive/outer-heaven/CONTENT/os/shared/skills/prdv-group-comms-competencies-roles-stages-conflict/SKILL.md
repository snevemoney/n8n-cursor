# Small-group comms: competencies, roles, stages, group conflict (clustered)

**Use when:** a prompt needs **small-group communication** — competencies (interpersonal process *used to facilitate* the group), roles, stages of development, typical challenges, techniques to improve, including **conflicts in group communication** — in the Saylor PRDV213 **Group Communication** school (6 h). Work, school, or volunteer. **Never send.**

**Do not use when:** reconstructing exams; **PRDV211** foundations *survey*; **PRDV212** one-to-one interpersonal (including interpersonal-conflict *language*); **PRDV214** organizational communication; **PRDV224** leadership and teams as a *management* course; **PRDV230** workplace conflict *policy*; **BUS641** strategic negotiations; inventing Tuckman (or any stage model) as if guest-named; promising ACE; **sending**.

This is **one** skill. Two live units, five CLOs. Loop: **competencies then roles then stages then challenges/group-conflict then improve.** Do not mint five unit-skills. Do not invent a sixth ethics CLO (ethics is a description overlay).

## 1. Procedure — six checks

Run in order. Stop if Check A says another school or send.

### Check A — Identity overlay (always)

| Prompt smells like | Hand off |
|---|---|
| "How communication works as a survey" | **PRDV211** |
| "One-to-one / we two had a fight" | **PRDV212** |
| "The whole organization as a system" | **PRDV214** |
| "Leadership and teams as management" | **PRDV224** |
| "Write a conflict-resolution *policy*" | **PRDV230** |
| "This small group / project team / volunteer committee" | **this skill** |
| "Send this" | **HITL — refuse send** |

**Primary goal:** a small group that can talk, develop, and handle conflict as a *group*. Named failure mode: treating it as a dyad, inventing stage names, or sending.

### Check B — Competencies as facilitation (when "what skills does this group need" is in play)

CLO 1. Unit 1.

**Identify specific competencies of the interpersonal communication process used in facilitating small group communication.** Live: interpersonal process is the *input* to facilitating the group, not a redirect to PRDV212 unless the prompt is actually one-to-one.

Shape: name the group, then the interpersonal competencies used to *facilitate* it. Do not collapse into 212.

### Check C — Roles (when "who does what in this group" is in play)

CLO 2. Unit 1.

**Explain the roles of people in small groups.**

Shape: roles in *this* small group. Do not dump an invented role roster unless a guest page named them. Leadership in the intro is an *outcome*, not a named role list.

### Check D — Stages (when "where is this team in its life" is in play)

CLO 3. Unit 1.

**Identify the stages of small group development.**

Shape: development over time. **Do not name Tuckman** (or any other model) as if live. Guest CLO says *stages*, not a brand.

### Check E — Challenges and group conflict (when "this meeting is a mess / we are fighting as a team" is in play)

CLO 4. Unit 2.

**Identify typical communication challenges that occur in small groups.** Live Unit 2 title: **Conflicts in Group Communication**.

Shape: challenge first, then whether it is group-level conflict (here) vs dyad (212) vs policy (230) vs negotiations (641). Stay group.

### Check F — Improve (when "how do we talk better as a team" is in play)

CLO 5. Units 1–2.

**Apply techniques to improve group communication.** Live intro: successful teamwork.

Shape: a technique that fits the named challenge/stage/role. **Refuse send.** Do not auto-calendar a retro.

## 2. Worked patterns (shape only)

**"Our project Slack is chaos."**
Check A (group, not dyad) then B (facilitation competencies) then E (challenges) then F (improve).

**"We two keep interrupting each other in the standup."**
If it is really a dyad inside the group: recap group frame, then hand the one-to-one piece to PRDV212. Stay here if the *group* is the patient.

**"What stage is this volunteer committee in?"**
Check D. Name *stages* as a job, not a branded model.

**"Write the team's conflict policy and email it."**
Check A → PRDV230 for policy. Recap E here as group-conflict *communication*. **Refuse send.**
