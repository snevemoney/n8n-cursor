# Financial literacy: goals, budget, credit, protect, global (clustered)

**Use when:** a prompt needs **personal financial literacy** — goals, budget/save, credit, debt, insurance, invest/retire, taxes, career/education money, scams, and a **global** overlay (poverty/unbanked, SRI/ESG, tech) — in the Saylor PRDV011 **Financial Literacy from a Global Perspective** school (22 h). **Advise-only.**

**Do not use when:** reconstructing exams; quoting **operator bank/runway FACT** (Personal CFO); **Wealth Manager** portfolio truth; **PRDV004** Excel budget as a spreadsheet course; filing taxes; opening a brokerage, loan, or insurance account; **moving money**; promising ACE; treating the 2012 S&P FinLit Survey as 2026 data; silently rewriting live ESG **government** into *governance*.

This is **one** skill. It is not ten unit skills. Ten live units, nine CLOs. Loop: **set goals → budget/save → credit → debt → insurance → invest/retire → taxes → career/education → scams → global trends.** Unit 1 has no dedicated CLO — keep it as a goals overlay on CLO 1.

## 1. Procedure — six checks

Run in order. Stop and hand off when Check A says the prompt is FACT money or a transaction.

### Check A — Identity overlay (always)

| Prompt smells like | Hand off |
|---|---|
| "What is *my* runway / quit date / real balance" | **Personal CFO** |
| "Portfolio FACT / allocation truth / what I actually hold" | **Wealth Manager** |
| "How do I make this budget *in Excel*" | **PRDV004** (CFO stub — do not overwrite) |
| "File this return / open this IRA / apply for this card / buy this fund" | **HITL / not this skill** |
| "How does household money *work* — budget, credit, insurance, scam, global" | **this skill** |

**Primary goal:** literate personal-money decisions in a global frame. Named failure mode: turning the course into a brokerage, treating 2012 survey gaps as the operator's identity, or quoting a worked example as FACT.

### Check B — Goals, budget, save (when "I don't know where the money goes" is in play)

CLO 1. Units 1–2.

**Outline how to prepare a personal budget and the financial benefits of saving.** Unit 1: establishing financial goals (overlay, not a tenth CLO). Unit 2: budgeting and saving.

Shape, not operator numbers: name the goal (time horizon, what "enough" means in the prompt's own terms), map inflows vs outflows, separate needs / wants / savings, name the benefit of saving (buffer, interest, optionality). If the prompt wants *their* dollars, stop and send to Personal CFO. Do not invent balances.

### Check C — Credit, borrow, debt (when "card / loan / bankruptcy" is in play)

CLO 2–3. Units 3–4.

**Define credit score** and the benefits and risks of consumer credit options (Unit 3: credit cards and consumer borrowing). **Explain common terms and conditions** consumers adopt when they borrow money and manage debt (Unit 4: loans and debt). Live description also names **personal bankruptcy** — treat it as a named topic in the school, not as legal advice and not as a filing.

Shape: APR / interest vs principal, minimum payment trap, secured vs unsecured, what a score *is* vs what it is not. Do not apply for credit. Do not recommend a specific issuer. HITL if they want to transact.

### Check D — Insurance, invest/retire, taxes (when "protect / grow / IRS" is in play)

CLO 4–6. Units 5–7.

**Discuss the types and terms of various insurance plans** consumers and businesses purchase to protect themselves, their families, and their property (Unit 5). **Describe strategies for increasing the value of savings and investments as you plan for retirement** (Unit 6: investing and retirement; live intro also names mutual funds and the stock market). **Explain the purpose, benefits, and costs of paying and managing taxes** (Unit 7: taxes and tax forms).

Shape: name the risk being transferred (insurance), the time horizon and diversification idea (invest/retire), and that taxes have purpose / benefits / *costs* (not "always minimize at all costs"). Do not file. Do not buy a product. Do not dump tax forms. Do not treat a course example ticker or fund as a recommendation.

### Check E — Career/education money (when "does this degree pay" is in play)

CLO 7. Unit 8.

**Discuss how career planning, education, and training affect your financial future.** This is the PD literacy overlay — opportunity cost of training, how credentials can change earning power, how that interacts with debt from Check C. Career Strategist owns the overlay. **Never send** employment email. Do not apply. Do not treat this as PRDV102 résumé writing.

### Check F — Scams and global overlay (when "fraud / ESG / unbanked" is in play)

CLO 8–9. Units 9–10.

**Identify frauds and scams and ways to safeguard your personal information and money** (Unit 9). **Discuss how technology, socially responsible investing (SRI), and the environmental, social, and government (ESG) movement have affected global financial markets** (Unit 10). Literacy/poverty/unbanked overlay from the intro stays here as *frame*, not as 2026 operator data.

Live ESG-G is **government**. Do not silently rewrite it as governance. If a later login resource corrects the acronym, that is a claim-vs-data update — not this harvest. Do not treat the 2012 S&P FinLit Survey as current statistics.

## 2. Worked patterns (shape only)

**"Should I start an emergency fund or pay the card?"**
Check A (literacy, not FACT) then B (goals/budget/save: buffer vs interest) then C (credit costs). Personal CFO if they want *their* numbers. Do not pick a dollar amount.

**"Is this Instagram stock tip a scam?"**
Check F first (safeguard information and money). Then D only if it is a real invest/retire question with a named horizon. Do not buy. Do not open a brokerage.

**"Does a master's raise my ceiling enough to justify the loan?"**
Check E (career/education money) then C (debt terms). Not a send-the-application. Not PRDV102.

**"Should I buy this ESG fund because the course mentioned sustainability?"**
Check F (SRI/ESG as *market overlay*, live wording government) then D (invest/retire strategies). Do not buy. Do not treat sustainability in the one-liner as a product pick.

**"Walk my actual tax return."**
Check A → HITL. This skill can name purpose/benefits/costs of paying and managing taxes. It does not file, and it does not dump forms.
