### Check B — Purpose and types (when "which format / résumé vs CV" is in play)

CLO 1. Unit 1.

**Describe the purpose and types of a professional résumé.** Indexed blurb: function/purpose; three traditional formats — **conventional**, **functional or skills-based**, **chronological**; also **CV** for academic / other positions that want a different document. Type depends on what you want to market and who the audience is.

Shape: résumé = concise, position-specific introduction (indexed CV article: often ≤1 page, altered per opening, usually with a cover letter). CV = longer academic/research record, updated frequently. If unsure which the employer wants, the indexed article says *ask* — do not guess-send.

### Check C — Key components, résumé and letter (when "what sections / how do I write the letter" is in play)

CLO 2. Unit 2.

**Identify the key components of résumés and cover letters.** Indexed blurb: choose categories that highlight significant accomplishments, professional skills, and other pertinent information; organize to showcase ability; design/format so the audience actually reads it; formulate the cover letter as a marketing tool.

Shape: contact, education, experience, skills — plus only the categories that serve *this* audience. Cover letter: what is being sent, to whom, who sent it, and why *this* person fits *this* opening. Do not dump a generic letter. Do not invent jobs.

### Check D — Market, tailor, living document (when "make it competitive / keywords / one version forever" is in play)

CLO 3. Unit 3.

**Describe ways to utilize your résumé and cover letters as effective marketing tools.** Live intro: living document; update after you land a job as skills and certifications change. Indexed blurb: action verbs, keywords, clear headings; edit/review (a friend can review); tailor to a specific opening; state how you provide the solution to the employer's hiring need.

Shape: one master living file, a *tailored* cut per opening. Keywords from the posting, not stuffed. Action verbs on real work. Quality pass before anyone sees it. **Still do not send** from this skill.
