# Org structure, change, control, future of management (clustered)

**Use when:** a prompt needs **organization structure, change, and the future of management** — a strong structure as the framework for goals and fit; what happens when structure changes; control; 21st-century change methods; technology and the future of work/management — in the Saylor PRDV229 **Organization Structure, Change, and the Future of Management** school (6 h). **Never send.** Building mode: never pitch.

## 1. Procedure — six checks

Run in order. Stop if Check A says another school or send.

### Check A — Identity overlay (always)

| Prompt smells like | Hand off |
|---|---|
| "Why do managers exist" | **PRDV221** |
| "Goals / vision / corporate strategy plan" | **PRDV227** |
| "Culture / ethics / diversity" | **PRDV223** |
| "How the org *talks*" | **PRDV214** |
| "Org chart, reorg, control, future of work" | **this skill** |
| "Send / pitch this reorg" | **HITL — refuse send** |

**Primary goal:** name how the place is structured, what a change does, and what is coming — without sending a reorg memo. Named failure mode: invented type/model names, treating 227’s change CLO as this course, or sending.

### Check B — Identify structures (when "what shape is this company" is in play)

CLO 1. Unit 1.

**Identify various organizational structures.** Guest named **no** types.

Shape: structures as a job. **Do not invent** functional, matrix, divisional, network, or holacracy. Unnamed overlay, same rule as Tuckman / SWOT.

### Check C — Structure vs strategy, performance, operations (when "the org chart is blocking the goal" is in play)

CLO 2. Unit 1 overlay.

**Explain ways that organizational structure affects strategy, performance, and operations.** Live intro: structure is the framework from which all goals are set; people and departments know where they fit.

Shape: structure → fit → goals/performance. If they only need the *goal*, hand 227 after this recap.

### Check D — Organizational control (when "how do we keep this aligned" is in play)

CLO 3.

**Explain methods of organizational control.** Guest named **no** control methods.

Shape: control as a job, not a surveillance how-to. Do not invent clan/bureaucratic/market names. Not 225 employee-conflict.

### Check E — Change in 21st-century trends (when "we're reorging" is in play)

CLO 4. Unit 2.

**Describe effective methods of change in 21st-century management trends.** Live intro: change from marketplace, competition, and/or new management theories. **Do not invent** Lewin or Kotter.

Shape: change methods as a job. 227’s “plan and execute change” CLO is overlay — **this** is the change *course*. If they want a strategy deck, 227 then return here for structure.

### Check F — Technology and the future of management/work (when "what is work becoming" is in play)

CLO 5. Units 3–4.

**Assess the role of technology in the future of management.** Live units: Management in the Future; The Future of Work.

Shape: assess, do not build. Not a hive-OS ticket. Not a product pitch. Building mode still holds.
