## 1. Procedure — answer an undergraduate intercultural-communication question in six checks

Run in order. Skip a check only if the user already constrained the frame.

### Check A — Identity / overlay (always)

**The course question:** is the user asking for a **five-context human-communication survey**, a **public speech**, a **PR campaign**, a **workplace memo**, a **graduate IB environment**, or **how culture changes this encounter**?

| Prompt smells like | Hand off |
|---|---|
| Interpersonal / group / org / mass as the *course* | **COMM100** |
| Prepare / deliver an ethical public speech | **COMM101** |
| PR campaigns / media relations | **COMM411** |
| Memo, negative news, channel/tone/feedback as bizcomm; culture as *one assignment* | **BUS210** |
| Graduate IB: entry mode, institutions, geopolitics; culture as *one unit* | **BUS613** |
| Compare practices, Hofstede, shock/bias/conflict, intercultural relationships, competence, workplace D&I | **this skill** |

**Primary goal:** become a better communicator in intercultural situations. That is not "pass Hofstede trivia," not "write the memo" (**BUS210**), and not "survey human communication" (**COMM100**).

**Named live failure modes:** collapsing greetings/time/eye contact into etiquette trivia; treating Hofstede as a personality test; treating culture shock as a travel diary; collapsing this course into bizcomm Unit 8 or graduate IB Unit 2.

### Check B — Compare practices + Hofstede (when "what is culture / how do we differ" is in play)

CLO 1 and CLO 2. Unit 1.

Live jobs: explain how communication meets personal needs (principles + models); **describe Hofstede dimensions**; **use those dimensions to compare cultures**; compare **verbal vs nonverbal** in intercultural contexts (storytelling, codes).

Map titles: The Study of Intercultural Communication; Why Study Intercultural Communication?; Starting with Culture; Culture and Communication; Culture Terminology; The Hofstede Model in Context; Verbal Communication; The Role of Storytelling in Different Cultures; Nonverbal Communication; Verbal and Nonverbal Codes.

**Encode the job** (name the dimension, compare two cultures, split verbal/nonverbal). Exact six-dimension list is a **login gap** if the readings differ from Grothe's standard set.

### Check C — Adapt: identity, bias, shock, conflict (when "we keep missing each other" is in play)

CLO 3, CLO 4, CLO 6. Unit 2.

Live jobs: stereotyping and its effects; common cultural biases and perceptions; **stages of culture shock**; **four ways** to deal with intercultural conflict; apply the **four-skills model** to conflict management.

Map titles: Self and Identity; Overcoming Biases; Culture Shock and Ethnocentrism; Conflict; Managing Conflict Styles on Diverse Teams; The Gift of Conflict; Conflict Resolution in Intercultural Communication.

Exact stage names and the four-skills model wording are **login gaps**. Encode the *job* (name the stage, pick a conflict move, apply a four-skill pass).

### Check D — Intercultural relationships (when "can we actually be close across cultures" is in play)

CLO 1, CLO 3, CLO 4. Unit 3.

Live jobs: types of intercultural relationships; foundations; barriers; competent vs incompetent relationship behaviors; the paradox of intercultural communication.

Map titles: Relationships; Making Friends Across Cultures; Intercultural Relationship Challenges; The Paradox of Intercultural Communication.

### Check E — Competence practices + culture-pairs + popular culture (when "what do I actually do / what does media do" is in play)

CLO 6 plus CLO 1/3/4. Unit 4.

Live jobs: explain and apply **intercultural communication competence** strategies; evaluate interpersonal outcomes between people of different cultures; analyze **popular culture and media** impact (high vs popular culture; tourism).

Named live culture-pair rows (map titles, not a dump): Chinese and American nonverbal; Chinese and American language; Scandinavian workplace; Koreans and American communication; UK and Arab CMC. Use as **worked examples**, not as the whole course.

### Check F — Workplace, education, healthcare, leadership, D&I (when the setting is an organization)

CLO 5 plus CLO 1/3/4/6. Unit 5.

Live jobs: how intercultural communication affects **business success**; interpersonal relationships at work; competent relationship practices in common work settings; **diversity and inclusion** practices across settings; **cultural intelligence**; problem-solving and leadership styles.

Map titles: Cultural Diversity Communication in the Workplace; Education; Healthcare; Business; How to Handle Cross-Cultural Differences in Business; Cultural Differences in Business Communication; Problem-Solving and Leadership Styles; Cultural Intelligence; Diversity and Inclusion; Belonging.

**Hand off BUS210** if the artifact is a workplace *message*. **Hand off BUS613** if the artifact is an *entry-mode / IB institutions* plan. Stay here for "this workplace encounter across cultures."
