# World history 1600–present: oceans first, then the right revolution, then the right war (clustered)

**Use when:** a task asks how to **place an event, person, ocean, or revolution** in the Saylor HIST103 **undergraduate world history 1600–present** school — which maritime system it belongs to, which revolution-type it is, which date-cluster (1648 / 1789 / 1914 / 1939 / 1991) does the job, how imperialism sits as an industrial-age structure, or how decolonization and the 21st century follow the Cold War.

HIST103's fourteen live units are one argument: **most survey students already have a European timeline; this course's pass is comparative global interactions — Indian Ocean and Africa and the Islamic world before the Atlantic is "founded," each revolution in its own seat, imperialism before 1914, decolonization after 1945.**

Run in order. Skip a check only if the user already constrained the frame.

**Primary goal:** a **comparative overview** of the last 400 years with **global interactions** first. That is not "Europe, then the rest," and not "everything is 1789 or 1914."

**Named live failure mode:** collapsing every maritime system into the **Atlantic**, every revolution into **1789**, every industrial change into **steam**, and every 20th-century rupture into **1914**.

### Check B — Oceans and early-modern centers (when "trade / empire / Islam / Africa / Atlantic" is in play)

CLOs 1–2. Units 2–5.

Name, in order:

1. **Which ocean.** Indian Ocean (Unit 2: existing India–Malacca–China–Japan circuits; Europeans *enter*) vs Atlantic (Unit 5: Columbian Exchange, mercantilism, plantation slavery) vs trans-Saharan (Unit 3: gold and captives *before and beside* the Atlantic).
2. **Which African seat.** Songhai / Swahili Coast / Elmina hinge (Unit 3) vs the Middle Passage *system* (Unit 5). Trans-Saharan ≠ Atlantic.
3. **Which Islamic empire.** Ottoman ≠ Safavid ≠ Mughal. Vienna 1529 is an Ottoman–Habsburg alarm in the parallel spine, not "Islam vs modernity."
4. **Reformation.** CLO 2 lives in **Unit 5** even though the unit title says Atlantic. Luther / Calvin / Henry VIII / **Council of Trent**. 1648 is consequence, not 1789.

**Hand off Unit 7** if they want 1776/1789. Stay here for "the world is already connected before the French Revolution."

### Check C — Revolution type (when "revolution / Enlightenment / industry / 1789" is in play)

CLOs 3–4. Units 6–7 (and 8–9 for industrial *life*).

Name the **type** before the **date**:

1. **Political / Atlantic revolutions** (Unit 7): Enlightenment → America 1776, France 1789, **Haiti 1791** (Toussaint). Haiti is inside this unit's OpenStax parallel even though the live title says Europe and North America. **Do not invent a live Latin America unit** (OpenStax ch.8 / Bolívar / San Martín / Brazil 1822 is **HIST362 overlay**).
2. **Scientific revolution** (CLO 4, SNLS folded this with industrial): method, astronomy/physics — not a factory.
3. **First Industrial** (Unit 6.3): Britain, mechanization, steam, loss of artisan control; Marx as *critique*.
4. **Second Industrial + high imperialism** (Unit 8): steel/chemicals/electricity as *means* to the Scramble. That is CLO 5, not CLO 3.

Quiz trap: **political ≠ industrial ≠ scientific.** **1789 ≠ 1648 ≠ 1914.**

**Hand off HIST362** for a revolutions *course*. **Hand off HIST363** for industry as a *course*. Stay here for "which revolution-type in the survey."

### Check D — Empire, labor, 1914 (when "imperialism / WWI / colonies" is in play)

CLOs 5–6. Units 8–10.

1. **High imperialism** is a **19th-century industrial-age structure** (raw materials, markets, steam, medicine, weapons; Scramble; spheres in China; U.S. Pacific). It *feeds* 1914. It is not a synonym for 1914.
2. **Life and labor** (Unit 9) is the city, coerced/semicoerced labor, diaspora, reform vs revolutionary ideology — not the steam-engine origin story and not the alliance chart.
3. **WWI** (Unit 10): alliances + naval race + Ottoman/Balkan nationalism; Franz Ferdinand as **trigger**; total war (colonies fight, homefront); 1917 Russia; Nov 1918; Versailles as **outcome**, not as WWII and not as 1950s independence.

Quiz trap: **1914 ≠ 1939.** Mandates after 1919 ≠ decolonization 1950s–60s.

### Check E — 1939, bipolarity, decolonization (when "Hitler / WWII / Cold War / independence" is in play)

CLOs 7–9. Units 11–13.

1. **Interwar** (Unit 11): why the 1930s produce the war (Depression, weak democracies, fascism/Nazism, Soviet formation, Japanese expansion). Totalitarian *origins*.
2. **WWII** (Unit 12): the war and the immediate reorder — theaters, Holocaust, U.S. and USSR as **superpowers**.
3. **Cold War + decolonization** (Unit 13): containment, NATO/Warsaw, PRC 1949, Korea, Non-Aligned (India/Egypt/Indonesia; Yugoslavia as non-orbit communist), 1950s–60s independence, 1989/1991.

Quiz trap: **decolonization is CLO 9's 1950s–60s**, not Versailles. Non-Aligned is a third path, not a secret bloc. Proxy war is how superpowers fight without direct annihilation.

### Check F — After 1991 (when "globalization / internet / climate / now" is in play)

CLO 10. Unit 14.

The pass is **new global markets and systems of trade and information exchange** (the live intro already named **physical and virtual networks**). Environment and unresolved violence are in the OpenStax/SNLS overlay — encode as *jobs*, do not freeze 2010 headlines as live doctrine. 1991 is not "the end of history" as this course's pass.

**Hand off GEOG101** for regions-as-geography. Stay here for "what replaced the Cold War map."

## 2. Typical traps (encode these; they are the pass)

- **1648 vs 1789 vs 1914.** Westphalia / French Revolution / WWI. Different units, different CLOs.
- **Atlantic vs Indian Ocean.** Triangular trade / Middle Passage is Unit 5. Porcelain, Malacca, VOC in Asia, Mughal/Qing circuits are Unit 2. Do not "start world trade in 1492."
- **Trans-Saharan vs Atlantic slave trades.** Unit 3 vs Unit 5.
- **Industrial vs political revolution.** Steam is not 1789. 1789 is not the Scramble.
- **First vs Second Industrial.** Textiles/steam vs steel/electricity/chemicals + high imperialism.
- **Reformation has no live unit title** but **CLO 2 still tests it** — Unit 5 overlay. Course brand is 1600–present; the 16th century still sits in the Atlantic-foundations seat.
- **Haiti is in Unit 7's parallel spine**; Bolívar is **not** a live unit. Do not file OpenStax ch.8 as Saylor Unit 8 (live Unit 8 is **Expansion in the Industrial Age**).
- **1914 trigger ≠ 1914 structure.** Assassination without alliances/imperial competition fails CLO 6.
- **1919 mandates ≠ 1950s–60s decolonization.**
- **Ottoman ≠ Safavid ≠ Mughal.**
- **SNLS 10 units / 86 h / LMS 31 / old title / 7-day unlimited cert** are **stale**.
- **ACE is N.** Do not promise SAYA credit. GEOG101 is the Social Science ACE neighbor.
