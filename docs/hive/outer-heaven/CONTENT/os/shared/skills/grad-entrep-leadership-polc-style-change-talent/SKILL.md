# Graduate entrepreneurial leadership: POLC the venture, then lead it (clustered)

**Use when:** a task asks how a founder or entrepreneurial leader should **run a venture of two or 200** — what POLC requires day to day, how to motivate a diverse/inclusive team, which leadership style fits, how to handle team dysfunction or performance, how to set goals and make decisions, what a talent-management focus looks like, or how to apply a named change model (Lewin) to the venture — in the Saylor BUS650 **graduate** entrepreneurial-leadership school.

**Do not use when:** the job is reconstructing or taking a Saylor (or any) exam, dumping Northouse/Yukl/Kotter/Goleman item banks, running **BUS208** undergraduate principles of management (POLC as *the course*), **BUS401** undergraduate strategic-leadership capstone, **BUS603** graduate managing people, **BUS640** graduate advanced OB, **BUS643** leading organizational change *as a course*, **BUS305** undergrad foundations of entrepreneurship, **BUS301** HRM as a course, treating the free 70% certificate as **MEI603** 80% proctored exams, promising ACE/transfer credit, inventing a live Maslow/Kotter/LMX stack, or inventing an MBA650 / MMKT650 / MMGT650 twin that is not in the catalog.

## 1. Procedure — answer a graduate entrepreneurial-leadership question in five checks

Run in order. Skip a check only if the user already constrained the frame.

### Check A — Identity, venture, overlay (always)

**The course question:** is the user asking for an **undergrad POLC course**, an **undergrad leadership capstone**, a **graduate people/OB course**, a **change course**, an **undergrad entrepreneurship survey**, or an **entrepreneurial-leadership overlay** that has to survive POLC, a named style, a motivated/D&I team, and a plan for dysfunction / talent / Lewin change?

Live test:

| Prompt smells like | Hand off |
|---|---|
| "What is POLC / Fayol / Mintzberg as intro management" | **BUS208** |
| "All managers are leaders / distinguish leadership vs management as the capstone" | **BUS401** |
| "Manage people at individual and group levels / HRM overlay as the course" | **BUS603** |
| "Advanced OB / leverage talent as consultant / leadership as Unit 1 of OB" | **BUS640** |
| "Kotter 8-step / leading organizational change as the course" | **BUS643** |
| "Write the business plan / launch the small venture as intro entrepreneurship" | **BUS305** |
| "Recruit/select/appraise as the HR cycle course" | **BUS301** (do not open that final) |
| "Timmons / ideation / entity formation / launch / seminar" | **BUS652 / BUS651 / BUS653 / BUS654** |
| "Lead this venture of 2 or 200 / POLC for the founder / which style / motivate this team / plan for dysfunction or Lewin change" | **this skill** |

**Primary goal:** a defensible way to lead **this venture** — POLC + style + motivated/D&I team + a plan for the named challenges. That is not "pass SHRM," not "write BUS305's plan," and not "define POLC" as intro (**BUS208**).

**Named live failure mode:** treating "entrepreneurial leadership" as generic POLC (the intro's POLC sentences are first-class *for the venture*, not a license to steal BUS208) or as a change-management course, without picking a style and a talent/D&I plan.

**Level check:** the undergrad entrepreneurship course is **BUS305**, not a missing BUS2xx "Entrepreneurial Leadership." If the prompt is actually BUS208 homework, **hand off BUS208**. If it is MEI603's proctored 80% exam, **refuse exam reconstruction**; this skill is the *content* overlay, not the degree exam.

### Check B — Role and POLC for the venture (when "I'm the founder / we are 2 people / we just hired 200 / how do I run the day" is in play)

CLO 2 overlay. Unit 1. Intro POLC.

POLC is the **day-to-day management of *your business***, not BUS208 as a course. Planning, organizing, leading, controlling — all four. Intro also names **behaviors and traits**. Live CLO 2 trait list (encode these; do not silently expand): **emotional intelligence, conflict management skills, communication skills, critical thinking.**

Name:

1. **How big is the venture** (intro's test: two or 200 — the job changes scale, not school).
2. **Which POLC function is actually failing** (planning vs organizing vs leading vs controlling).
3. **Which named trait** is missing (EI / conflict / comms / critical thinking).

Carpenter overlay (summarize): entrepreneurship is a *thread* through POLC, not a 16-chapter dump. **Hand off BUS208** for PoM-as-a-course. Stay here for "this founder, this POLC function, this trait."

### Check C — Motivate the team and D&I (when "nobody will follow / they are burned out / we hired unlike ourselves" is in play)

Unit 2. Intro verbs: **motivate a team** and **ensure diversity and inclusion**. Both.

Encode **what will stall this venture if the team is unmotivated or excluded** — not a Maslow recitation.

Do **not** require Herzberg/Expectancy as live CLO language. **Hand off BUS603 / BUS640** for motivation as a *people/OB course*. **Hand off BUS209** for undergrad OB. Stay here for "this entrepreneurial team, this D&I constraint."

### Check D — Style (when "should I be the servant / they want a transformer / we only transact" is in play)

CLO 1. Unit 3.

**Named public set** (encode these; do not silently expand): intro **servant leadership** and **authentic leadership**; CLO **transactional** and **transformational**. The CLO verb is **analyze the differences** between leadership and management theories.

Encode **which named style fits this venture of 2 or 200, and what it is not**. Servant/authentic are how the intro says you *motivate*. Transactional/transformational are how the CLO says you *tell theories apart*.

**Hand off BUS401** if they want the undergrad capstone distinction as *the course*. **Hand off BUS640 Unit 1** for advanced-OB leadership. Stay here for "this venture, this named style."

Do **not** freeze Fiedler / LMX / laissez-faire as live Saylor doctrine. Carpenter names more — that is parallel.

### Check E — Challenges: dysfunction, talent, Lewin change (when "the team is broken / we can't hire / we have to change" is in play)

CLO 3 + CLO 4 + CLO 5. Unit 4. Intro overlay: **decisions**, **performance issues**, **set goals**.

**Named live challenge set:** **team dysfunction** and **change management** (CLO 3 — *propose a plan*). **Talent management focus** (CLO 4 — *identify factors*). **Lewin's Model** applied to an **entrepreneurial venture** (CLO 5 — *apply*).

Encode:

- What is the actual challenge (dysfunction vs performance vs a change vs a talent gap)?
- What is the **plan** (CLO 3's verb)?
- If it is change: use **Lewin** as the named model. Do **not** dump Kotter 8-step as live CLO language.
- If it is talent: name the *focus* the entrepreneurial leader enhances — not BUS301's HR cycle.

**Hand off BUS643** the moment they want leading organizational change *as a course*. **Hand off BUS301** for HRM as a course (**do not open that final**). Stay here for "this founder, this plan, this Lewin move."

## 2. Worked patterns (shape only — not exam items)

**"We are three founders and just hired our first twenty people — which style, and how do I POLC this?"**
Check A (venture of ~20, between 2 and 200) → Check B (which POLC function is new — usually organizing + controlling) → Check D (servant/authentic for motivation; transactional vs transformational as the theory contrast) → Check C (D&I as the team grows) → Check E (talent-management *focus*, not BUS301). Do not turn it into BUS208's survey or BUS305's business plan.

**"The team is dysfunctional and we have to change the product."**
Check E (plan for dysfunction + Lewin on the *venture*) → Check C (motivation/D&I) → Check D (which style can carry the unfreeze). **Hand off BUS643** if they want change *as the course*. Stay here for the founder's plan.

**"Is this a people-management problem or an entrepreneurial-leadership problem?"**
Check A. If the job is MBA-core managing people / HRM overlay, **hand off BUS603**. If the job is advanced OB, **hand off BUS640**. If the job is POLC + style + venture challenges, stay here.

**"MEI603 / MBA650 / MMKT650 / MMGT650."**
Only **MEI603** exists as this title-family. MBA609 is Leadership Capstone. MMGT603 is Managing People. Do not invent twins.
