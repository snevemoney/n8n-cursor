# Viral content scout (Demkinn #2)

Owner: Creative Studio (Researcher may feed candidates). Gap → runnable.

## When
Daily content hunt, or Publishing Engine needs hooks.

## Steps
1. Scan for novelty, demos, big numbers, controversy, visuals (X bookmarks, Watch Later, research packets).
2. Score each candidate 1–5 on: **Novelty × visual × money × controversy × simplicity × emotion**.
3. Rank **top 5 shareable** — not the most important stories. Importance stays on Researcher’s news track.
4. Write a dated score sheet under `/workspace/research/packets/` or `~/.grokbot/research/viral-scout/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
5. Hand top angles to Publishing Engine (and Remotion if the hook is a coded walkthrough / map / animate). Draft only — HITL before public post.

## Done when
Score sheet + top 5 list exists as an artifact.

## Never
- Invent engagement numbers
- Auto-post
- Mint agents
- Confuse “important” with “shareable”
