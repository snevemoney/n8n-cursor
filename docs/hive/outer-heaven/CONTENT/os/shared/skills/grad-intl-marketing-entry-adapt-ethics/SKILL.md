# Graduate international marketing: std vs adapt, select, enter, track (clustered)

**Use when:** a task asks how a firm should **apply marketing across borders** — why enter and which market, whether to standardize or adapt under cultural and economic differences, how an international marketing department should sit, how research and MIS feed STP, which trend (including internet/digital) the department must absorb, which alternative entry *plan* to evaluate, or how to track the effort with ROI / KPIs / Google Analytics so an integrated international marketing plan (product, service, company, e-commerce platform, or individual) actually lands — in the Saylor BUS615 **graduate** international-marketing school.

**Do not use when:** the job is reconstructing or taking a Saylor (or any) exam, dumping Cateora/Keegan/Hofstede/CAGE/GA item banks, running **BUS203** undergraduate principles of marketing (4Ps / STP / advertising-vs-sales as intro), **BUS403** negotiations and conflict (not a marketing course), **BUS502** undergrad strategic-marketing overlay, **BUS602** graduate marketing management (mix under mission/vision as a *course*), **BUS612** graduate data-driven communications (same 32 h trap), **BUS613** graduate advanced IB (environment / institutions / IB entry modes), **BUS614** international finance, **BUS630–634** specialty stack (CB / brand / digital / sales / research-methods), **COMM311** intercultural comms as a course, treating the free 70% certificate as **MBA615** or **MMKT606** 80% proctored exams, promising ACE/transfer credit, or inventing a live Hofstede/export-JV-FDI stack.

This is **one** skill. It is not "Unit 1 skill + Unit 2 skill…". BUS615's seven live units are one argument: **even smart global firms mis-enter; international marketing is deciding why/which, std-vs-adapt under culture, putting the department around that choice, researching the select, absorbing the trend, evaluating entry plans, and tracking so the integrated plan lands.**

## 1. Procedure — answer a graduate international-marketing question in six checks

Run in order. Skip a check only if the user already constrained the frame.

### Check A — Identity, why/which, overlay (always)

**The course question:** is the user asking for an **intro PoM definition**, a **negotiations** answer, a **domestic marketing-management plan**, an **IB-environment** read, a **data story**, a **digital/ad ops** course, or an **international marketing plan** that has to pick a market, std-vs-adapt the mix, and track entry?

Live test:

| Prompt smells like | Hand off |
|---|---|
| "What is marketing / 4Ps / advertising vs sales as intro" | **BUS203** |
| "BATNA / Thomas-Kilmann / ADR / workplace conflict" | **BUS403** (not marketing) |
| "Spend the mix under mission/vision as the marketing *manager*" | **BUS602** |
| "SWOT/PESTEL + five-unit undergrad plan overlay" | **BUS502** |
| "Institutions, trade, BOP/SWIFT, IB entry *modes* as IB" | **BUS613** |
| "FX / cross-border finance as a course" | **BUS614** |
| "Slide narrative / viz / data comms as a course" | **BUS612** |
| "Performance media / ad ops / digital as a *course*" | **BUS632** |
| "CB lab / brand architecture / sales force / research methods as a *course*" | **BUS630 / 631 / 633 / 634** |
| "Intercultural message as a comms course" | **COMM311** |
| "Why this foreign market, std vs adapt, department, research-select, entry *plan*, ROI/KPI/GA" | **this skill** |

**Primary goal:** an integrated international marketing plan you can **track**. That is not "pass Google Analytics," not "define the 4Ps" (**BUS203**), and not "stand up the IB environment" (**BUS613**).

**Named live failure mode:** smart global companies still make **major missteps** entering foreign markets (intro names Amazon, Toyota, Nike as hooks — do not dump the cases).

**Named live market set** (encode these; do not silently drop B2B/B2G): **B2C, C2C, B2B, B2G.** Live intro: much is written about B2C/C2C; comparatively little on B2B/B2G; international buyers differ in attitude and receptivity.

**Level check:** if the prompt is actually undergrad homework in BUS203's seven units, **hand off BUS203** and recap only. If it is MBA615's / MMKT606's proctored 80% exam, **refuse exam reconstruction**; this skill is the *content* overlay, not the degree exam.

### Check B — Standardization vs adaptation under culture (when "same global campaign / localize the offer / this culture will reject it" is in play)

CLO 2. Units 1–2, 5 overlay.

The CLO is **assess advantages and disadvantages of standardization and adaptation**. Both sides. Culture (Unit 2) and trends (Unit 5) change the calculus; they do not replace it.

Encode:

1. **What must be the same** (brand signal, cost, speed, one department campaign) vs **what must move** (offer, price/policy, advertising claim, channel) for *this* cultural and economic reality.
2. Do **not** freeze Hofstede / Trompenaars / CAGE as live CLO language. Name the *job* (the mix has to land here) and leave branded dimension names until login.
3. Course Description payload: **dynamic communications, international advertising, product pricing and policies, customers with different cultural and economic realities.**

**Hand off COMM311** for intercultural comms as a course. **Hand off BUS613 Unit 2** for language-and-culture as IB (communication *and negotiation*). **Hand off BUS630** for CB as a course. Stay here for "this culture, this std-vs-adapt marketing mix."

### Check C — International marketing department + named customer models (when "who decides / HQ vs local / which canvas / 4Cs" is in play)

CLO 4. Unit 3.

The department is first-class. Live intro: internet/digital are **critical strategic elements for every company's marketing department**. Carpenter overlay (summarize): centralized vs decentralized decision rights — scale/consistency vs local knowledge. Confirm live labels; encode the *job* even if the org words are unconfirmed.

**Named live models — encode the names, do not expand until login:**

- Customer Value Chain
- Customer Value Proposition Canvas
- the 4Cs of marketing
- the 4Cs of marketing communications

Those are **two different 4Cs**. Do not collapse them. Do not guess Lauterborn vs another vocabulary as Saylor doctrine.

**Hand off BUS602** if they want marketing nested in firm strategic planning as a *course*. **Hand off BUS631** for brand architecture as a *course*. Stay here for "this international marketing department, these named models for international offerings."

### Check D — Research, MIS, STP-select (when "which country / which segment / we don't have data on B2B buyers" is in play)

CLO 1 (which), 5, 6. Unit 4.

Two nouns in CLO 5: **marketing research** *and* **marketing information systems**. CLO 6: **choose STP** for entering an international market. Selection is not a fishing trip (Unit 1's "growing foreign economies" is the pull, not the pick).

Live intro already named the research gap: B2B/B2G under-written; different receptivity. Encode "you cannot enter a market you have not selected with research."

**Hand off BUS634** for marketing-research methods / application as a *course*. **Hand off BUS203** for intro research process. Stay here for "this international select, this MIS-fed plan."

### Check E — Trends, then alternative entry plans (when "digital changes everything / export vs partner / how do we enter" is in play)

CLO 2 overlay + CLO 7–8. Units 5–6.

**Trends (Unit 5).** Live intro: internet and digital evolving rapidly, department-level strategy. Do **not** guess a TikTok/programmatic/performance-media stack as Saylor doctrine. **Hand off BUS632** the moment the prompt is digital/ad as a *course* (also 32 h — do not borrow those hours). **Hand off BUS612** if they want the data picture as a course.

**Entry plans (Unit 6).** CLO 8: **evaluate alternative market entry *plans***. The noun is *plans*, not BUS613's IB *modes*. OpenStax 7.3 overlay (summarize, unconfirmed as live list): export vs joint venture vs investment — investment/control/profit trade-offs. BUS613 search-indexed residue named direct exporting / M&A / partnering — **do not file those as this course's list** until login.

**Hand off BUS613 Unit 6** for IB market-entry as environment/mode/supply-chain interdependence. **Hand off BUS614** if the constraint is finance. Stay here for "this marketing entry plan, these alternatives, this mix that has to land."

### Check F — Track, then write the integrated plan (when "did it work / KPI / GA / write the international plan" is in play)

CLO 3, 9, 10–11. Unit 7 (capstone).

**Track (CLO 9).** Live tool list: **ROI, KPIs, or Google Analytics**. Encode those three. Not a GA cert. Not BUS612's dashboard-as-comms course.

**Thinking models (CLO 3).** Choose models against real-world **successes and failures**. The intro named missteps so the model has to explain a failure — do not dump Amazon/Toyota/Nike cases.

**The plan (CLO 10–11).** Identify the **main elements** of an integrated international marketing plan for a **product, service, company, e-commerce platform, or individual**, then **design your own**. The plan is the course product.

**Hand off BUS602** for the domestic marketing-management plan nested in mission/vision. **Hand off BUS502** for the undergrad five-unit overlay. Stay here for "this international entry plan, these tracking tools."
