# Business-proficient ESL: correspondence genres, then professional English, then a polite email (clustered)

**Use when:** a task asks how to place a business-proficient (C2) ESL question in the Saylor ESL005 school — business correspondence genres, professional communication in English business contexts, or polite error-free business emails.

**Do not use when:** ESL001–004; ESL006 healthcare; ENGL*; BUS*; ESLHub; CS120; sitting ACE; exam reconstruction; sending.

This is **one** skill. Three live units, one argument: **most students already have ESL004 punctuation + academic sources; this course pass is C2 business writing — you can name a correspondence genre, use professional English at work, and write a polite error-free business email. You do not sit BUS or ENGL.**
