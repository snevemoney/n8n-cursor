# Global justice: rights context, then debates, then compatibility (clustered)

**Use when:** a task asks how to **place a global-justice question** in the Saylor POLSC402 school — human-rights context, which justice debate, which conception of the self, or whether global and justice are compatible.

**Do not use when:** POLSC401 policy ethics; POLSC101 IR intro; PHIL103 Rawls/Kant as the course; POLSC232 US system; ENGL; PSYCH; exam reconstruction.

This is **one** skill. Seven live units, one argument: **most students already have a human-rights slogan or a domestic-justice seminar; this course's pass is whether justice can be global — context and origins first, then political theory, then three named debates, then compatibility in theory and practice.**

## 0. Hygiene

- Label **school**. Undergraduate **global justice** (Saylor POLSC402). **31 h / 3.1 CEU.** LMS **50** (`/course/polsc402`). ACE **N**. Cert **70% / 14-day / max 3**. Syllabus **2025-10-03 15:28**. No guest-visible textbook.
- Seven **live** units as in PACKET. Six **live** CLOs.

## 1. Procedure — six checks

### Check A — Identity

| Prompt smells like | Hand off |
|---|---|
| Ethics of a domestic/foreign *policy* | **POLSC401** |
| IR as intro-survey | **POLSC101** |
| Rawls/Kant as PHIL103 | **PHIL103** |
| Take the final | **refuse** |
| Can justice be global / who is the self across borders | **this skill** |

### Check B — Context (Units 1–2)

Human-rights context: conceptual + international historical + western vs non-western. Origins of contemporary justice and rights discourse.

### Check C — Theory (Unit 3)

Natural order vs utilitarian. Nationalist vs cosmopolitan. Conceptions of the **self** and matching theories of justice. Global distributive justice: arguments for and against.

### Check D — Three debates (Units 4–6)

Do not collapse:
- Universal vs relative (empowerment, agency)
- Individual vs collective (conflicting claims)
- Civil-political vs ESCR; needs vs rights (participation)

### Check E — Compatibility (Unit 7)

Are *global* and *justice* compatible in theory **and** practice? Reconsider theory in light of agency, conflicting pursuits, needs vs rights. Not a yes-slogan.

### Check F — Neighbors

Western/non-western analysis is this SKU (CLO 5), not GEOG101 regions.

## 2. Typical traps

- Human rights as US Bill of Rights.
- Rawls as this whole map.
- POLSC401 Unit 7 reused.
- Three debates collapsed into "cultural relativism."
- Distributive justice as domestic only.
- ACE invented. 31 h ignored.

## 3. Refuse

Exam reconstruction; inventing SAYA; sitting the final; ENGL/PSYCH harvest.

## 4. Adjacent

POLSC401, POLSC101, PHIL103, POLSC232, ACE sitting (none).

## 5. Output shape

1. Name the school (POLSC402 global justice) and what it is not.
2. Name the **unit / debate**.
3. If cosmopolitan/nationalist: name the **self**.
4. Do not invent ACE. Do not sit the final.
