# Graduate digital marketing: mix, SEM, campaign (clustered)

**Use when:** a marketer needs **digital strategy and campaign** — concepts, situational analysis, multiplatform mix, SEM, monitoring — in Saylor BUS632.

**Do not use when:** BUS203 undergrad; BUS602 management; BUS630 CB; BUS631 brand; BUS633 sales; BUS634 research; BUS615 international; MMKT604 exam; ACE promises; buying ads; posting as operator; exam reconstruction; spend.

This is **one** skill. Ten live units fold into **concepts → situation → mix → channels (UX, viral/content, social, SEM, YouTube, email) → monitor**. Five CLOs. **SEM is its own CLO** — do not fold it into the mix.

## 0. Hygiene

- Label **school**. Graduate MMKT digital marketing and advertising (Saylor BUS632). LMS **1266** (`/course/bus632`). Twin **MMKT604**. **32 h / 3.2 CEU.** Cert **70% / 14-day / max 3**. ACE **N**. Syllabus page **91025** (Friday, October 3, 2025, 12:56 PM). AI zone **102875**.
- Ten **live** units: Introduction to Digital Marketing and Advertising; Situational Analysis in the Online Environment; Creating a Digital Marketing Mix; User Experience and User Interface Design; Viral Marketing and Content Marketing; Social Media and Influencers; Search Engine Marketing; YouTube Marketing; Email Marketing; Monitoring and Evaluation.
- Five **live** CLOs.
- Do not invent MBA632 / MEI632 / MMGT632. Do not sit MMKT604. Do not open BUS607. Guest/curl only. **Never send. Never buy ads.**

**One-liner (live):** In this graduate-level course, learn how to build digital marketing strategies, such as influencer marketing, viral marketing, and social media advertising, to create a strong online presence for your business.

### Check B — Concepts (CLO 1; Unit 1)

Apply **key digital marketing concepts and strategies** to business success. Strategy, not spend.

### Check C — Situation (CLO 2; Unit 2)

Situational analysis of **online and offline** variables. Digital does not ignore offline.

### Check D — Mix and channels (CLO 3; Units 3–6, 8–9)

Develop an **e-marketing campaign** with a **multiplatform** strategy. Unit seats: mix; UX/UI; viral/content; social/influencers; YouTube; email. Description names influencer marketing, viral marketing, and social media advertising. Still not a send.

### Check E — SEM (CLO 4; Unit 7)

**Design a search engine marketing strategy.** Own CLO. Do not fold into D. Designing ≠ buying keywords.

### Check F — Monitor (CLO 5; Unit 10)

Evaluate campaign performance and collect insights for decision-making. Do not buy ads to “test.”
