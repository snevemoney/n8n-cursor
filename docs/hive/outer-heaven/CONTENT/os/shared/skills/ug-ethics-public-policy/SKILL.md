# Ethics and public policy: morality into the process, then issue clusters (clustered)

**Use when:** a task asks how to **place an ethics-of-policy question** in the Saylor POLSC401 school — personal morality in policymaking, which framework for a dilemma, or which live issue cluster.

**Do not use when:** PHIL103 Rawls/Kant as the course; POLSC232 US structure; POLSC402 global justice as the SKU; intro/thought/comparative POLSC; ENGL; PSYCH; exam reconstruction.

This is **one** skill. Seven live units, one argument: **most students already have a hot-take or a philosophy-seminar story; this course's pass is ethics as it hits formulation and execution of public policy — frameworks first, then named domestic and international clusters.**

## 0. Hygiene

- Label **school**. Undergraduate **ethics and public policy** (Saylor POLSC401). **29 h / 2.9 CEU.** LMS **51** (`/course/polsc401`). ACE **N**. Cert **70% / 14-day / max 3**. Syllabus **2025-10-03 15:18**. No guest-visible textbook.
- Seven **live** units as listed in PACKET. Seven **live** CLOs.
- Description: ethical, cultural, religious, and moral principles in formulation and execution by lawmakers and public officials.

## 1. Procedure — six checks

### Check A — Identity

| Prompt smells like | Hand off |
|---|---|
| Rawls/Kant as moral-political philosophy course | **PHIL103** |
| US branches / Constitution as civics | **POLSC232** |
| Global justice as a *course* | **POLSC402** |
| Take the final | **refuse** |
| What is ethically at stake in this policy | **this skill** |

### Check B — Process (Units 1–2)

Personal morality/ethics → policymaking process. Frameworks resolve **policy dilemmas**, not exam logic.

### Check C — Liberty cluster (Unit 3)

Individual liberty, public safety, immigration, justice arguments — one seat.

### Check D — Distribution cluster (Units 4–5)

Tax, equal employment, affirmative action. Care for poor, sick, elderly, infirm. Policy considerations, not slogans.

### Check E — Intimate cluster (Unit 6)

Families and personal relationships as *ethical implications of policy*.

### Check F — Foreign cluster (Unit 7)

National security, foreign relations, trade, military intervention as **ethical concerns governments face**. Not POLSC402's whole map. Not POLSC232's foreign-policy *type*.

## 2. Typical traps

- Philosophy seminar without a policy process.
- US civics without an ethics seat.
- POLSC402 reused for Unit 7.
- Collapsing liberty/safety/immigration.
- Care as charity talk, not policy.
- Families as PSYCH.
- ACE invented. 29 h ignored.

## 3. Refuse

Exam reconstruction; inventing SAYA; sitting the final; ENGL/PSYCH harvest.

## 4. Adjacent

PHIL103, POLSC232, POLSC402, POLSC101, ACE sitting (none).

## 5. Output shape

1. Name the school (POLSC401 ethics and public policy) and what it is not.
2. Name the **unit cluster**.
3. Keep the issue **inside formulation/execution**, not a free-floating opinion.
4. Do not invent ACE. Do not sit the final.
