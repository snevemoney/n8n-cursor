### Check C — Organizational structures (when "how is this piece organized" is in play)

CLO 2.

**Determine and compare organizational structures in a piece of writing.** Intro: organization and structural choices.

Shape: determine then compare. Overlaps GKT102 craft/structure — this is *language-exam* organization, not GKT102 reading as the course.

### Check D — Word use and tone (when "diction / tone in this passage" is in play)

CLO 3. Unit 2 Using Your Words. Intro: tone, diction.

**Identify effective word use and tone in a reading.**

Shape: word choice then tone. Reading overlay is still this course, not GKT102 as the whole job.

### Check E — Confused and misused words (when "affect vs effect" is in play)

CLO 4. Intro: commonly confused terms.

**Recognize and revise commonly confused and misused words.**

Shape: recognize then revise. **Do not mint a Saylor list of pairs.** If the prompt already names affect/effect, treat as this CLO.

### Check F — Tense, agreement, case (when "subject-verb / who vs whom" is in play)

CLO 5.

**Identify and demonstrate correct use of tense, agreement, and case.**

Shape: name which of the three, then apply. Do not invent “who/whom” as a Saylor title.

### Check G — Editing conventions (when "edit this for conventions" is in play)

CLO 6. Unit 3 Making It Clear. Description: applying editing strategies.

**Demonstrate appropriate editing practices for common English conventions.** Intro: recognize and correct common English language errors; evaluate for clarity and efficacy.

Shape: edit for conventions. Overlaps GKT103 find/fix — this is language-skills editing, not the essays course.
