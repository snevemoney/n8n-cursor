# Site brief

Turn a fuzzy website ask into a one-page brief before any design or scaffold.

## Steps
1. Confirm audience, job-to-be-done, and success metric (lead, book, buy, trust).
2. List required pages (max 7 for Launch Site) and what each must do.
3. Name CMS owner (client vs builder) and content readiness (copy exists? images?).
4. Note integrations: booking, forms, analytics, email, payments.
5. Capture constraints: deadline, brand assets, must-keep URLs, accessibility bar.
6. Output a one-page brief: Audience · JTBD · Pages · CMS owner · Integrations · Constraints · Out of scope.
7. Get operator/client confirm before stack-pick or design-tokens.

## Anti-patterns
- Designing in Figma/v0 before the brief
- Promising CMS features without an owner
