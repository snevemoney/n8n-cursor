# BizDoc company case method (for equity research)

Use on every serious name before BUY/WATCH/HOLD/REDUCE. Label every claim FACT / PRESS / ANALYSIS / SOCIAL. YouTube case studies = SOCIAL until verified in IR/10-K/8-K. Never buy from YouTube alone. Do not invent prices. Advise-only.

## Procedure

1. **Reframe (1 sentence)** — "This is a ____ with a ____ attached." Name the economic core.
2. **Origin only as scaffolding** — short founding beat; jump to the model that still drives cash.
3. **Real customer** — who pays, who consumes, who is ignored, who is best for unit economics.
4. **Ops asset vs engine asset** — two columns. Ask: if ops froze, what would lenders still collateralize?
5. **Unit economics (sourced only)** — engine % of revenue; vs op income; growth vs commodity line. Tag SOCIAL/FACT; unknown → pull IR.
6. **Ownership / control / partner exclusivity** — hard assets, board control, exclusive partners, contract end-dates.
7. **Moat test** — platform, switching costs, culture/selection, exclusive partner, brand+channel.
8. **Liability / redemption / CapEx** — who controls redemption or spend, not just issuance/sales.
9. **Expect vs reality** — Street narrative vs print/orders/backlog. Ranges, not fake point targets.
10. **Peer compare on one hard metric** — same yardstick only.
11. **Byproduct P&L** — revenue/collateral on capacity already paid for.
12. **Wealth OS scorecard** — expect vs reality | moat | liability | partner exclusivity | byproduct P&L.
13. **IR verification gate** — list docs to promote SOCIAL → FACT.
14. **Call line** — BUY|WATCH|HOLD|REDUCE|EXIT|NO_ACTION + size advice (HITL). Never "YouTube buy."

## Hard rules
SOCIAL until IR · no YouTube buys · no invented prices · map every case to Wealth OS five lenses · advise-only.
