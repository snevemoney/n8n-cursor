### Check B — Markets, banking, rates (Units 1–3, CLO 1–3)

Name which market (money, bond, equity), which intermediary/regulation (Unit 2 Banking), and what interest-rate **structure/determination** is doing in capital markets. Do not invent a live yield-curve doctrine.

### Check C — Equity as allocation, then HF/PE (Units 4–5, CLO 4–5)

Equity CLO is functioning vs other markets and **allocating resources**, not "value this stock". Institutional CLO: importance + strategies. Live unit names **hedge funds and private equity**. Do not silently add pensions/SWFs as CLO language until login.

### Check D — Crises as genesis and phases (Unit 6, CLO 6)

Encode the *arc*, not a frozen 2008/ERM item bank. Named cases are likely and unconfirmed. Not personal-CFO "sell everything."

### Check E — FX you calculate, then Eurozone (Units 7–8, CLO 7–8)

CLO 7: how FX **works** and **calculate** different types of exchange rates. OpenStax overlay (summarize): spot; hedge as a fee to lock a future rate; transaction vs economic exposure. Confirm live rate types on login.

CLO 8: Eurozone's role in today's global markets. Unit 8 is **EMU**. Do not replace with "BRICS." Hand off ECON102 for FX-as-macro diagrams; BUS613 Unit 4 if they only need plumbing for entry.

### Check F — Derivatives that hedge or insure, then trends (Units 9–10, CLO 9–10)

CLO 9: **compare** products; uses = **hedging and insuring**. Product names (forward/future/option/swap) likely and unconfirmed — name the *job*.

CLO 10: identify trends. Park catalog emerging economies here. Operator Bitcoin sitting is ECON135/PRDV151, not automatic Unit 10 doctrine.
