# Graduate launch: ethics, strategy, change, grow (clustered)

**Use when:** a founder is past the business-plan phase and needs **launch and growth** — ethics/CSR, innovation/growth plans, strategic plans (vision/mission), a start-up change-management plan, growth methods (marketing plan / financing for growth) — in Saylor BUS653.

**Do not use when:** BUS652 ideation; BUS651 law; BUS650 leadership; BUS654 seminar; BUS305 undergrad; BUS401 strategy-as-course; MEI609 exam; ACE promises; fundraising execution; exam reconstruction; spend/money movement.

This is **one** skill. Five units = **ethics → innovation/growth → strategy → change/decision → grow.** Five CLOs. **Launch is not BUS652 vet, not BUS651 incorporate, not BUS654 pitch-send.**

### Check B — Ethics / CSR (CLO 1; Unit 1)

Methods that can be implemented into an entrepreneurial venture to focus on ethics and corporate social responsibility. Diversity/hiring is intro-named. Not legal advice (BUS651).

### Check C — Innovation and growth plans (CLO 2; Unit 2)

Formulate plans for organizational innovation and growth. Lifecycles / strategy-by-stage are intro-named. Not BUS604 as this course.

### Check D — Strategic plans (CLO 3; Unit 3)

Develop and design strategic plans. Vision and mission are intro-named tools. Not BUS401 corporate strategy-as-the-course.

### Check E — Change (CLO 4; Unit 4)

Propose a change-management plan for a **new start-up**. Decision-making is in the unit title. Named for a start-up, not a mature corp reorg dump.

### Check F — Growth methods (CLO 5; Unit 5)

Determine growth strategy methods. Marketing plan and financing for growth are intro-named. **Methods, not a spend card / fundraise execution.**

## 2. Worked patterns (shape only)

**We have a plan. Now what?** B → C → D → E → F. Not “vet the idea” (BUS652). Not “form the LLC” (BUS651). Not “send the pitch” (BUS654 + HITL).

**How do we grow?** F, with C and D as the plan layer. Financing-for-growth is a **method**, not money movement.

**BUS653 or BUS654?** Launch/grow operations → this skill. Founder comms / EQ / plan+pitch analysis after 650–653 → BUS654.
