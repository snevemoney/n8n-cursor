# Value based pricing ladder

Sources: Nate value-based pricing; youtube-ai-implement P1; Money Desk CAD bands.

## When
Packaging AI Partner audit or retainer tiers.

## Do
1. Price from outcome / year-1 value band, not hours only.
2. Three tiers, 30-day milestones, V2 backlog; maintenance is not new features.
3. Align CAD bands with Money Desk SoT. Educator 3-10K retainers stay UNVERIFIED.
4. Lead with receipts, not portfolio screenshots.

## Don't
- Quote Nate/Mert/Liam income as ours
- Sell CUT retainer to other ecom sellers (HOLD)
- Auto-send proposals
