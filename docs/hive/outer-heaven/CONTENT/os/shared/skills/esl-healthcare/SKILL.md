Describe **structure, purpose, and function of healthcare systems**. Distinguish **provider types and their roles**. Not a policy paper.

### Check D — Written tasks and etiquette (CLO 1, 6–7; Unit 5)

**Reading and writing in a medicine context.** **Define healthcare-related written tasks.** **Medical office etiquette.** Do not send the note or email.
