# Sales literacy: relationships, ethics, presentation shape, team — never sell (clustered)

**Use when:** a prompt needs **professional-sales literacy** — relationship types, comms skills, ethical vs unethical behavior, develop professional relationships, *shape* a persuasive sales presentation, sales-team member skills, lead a sales team — in the Saylor PRDV217 **Introduction to Sales** school (22 h). **Never send. Never pitch. Never sell.** Operator building mode: no selling products or services until he says so.

**Do not use when:** reconstructing exams; **actually selling** or outbound; **PRDV008** presentations as a course; **PRDV213** small-group comms; **PRDV224** leadership and teams as management; **Product GTM** live hunt; promising ACE; **sending** a deck or email; treating hive factory work as a sales signal.

This is **one** skill. Five live units, seven CLOs. Loop: **relationship types then comms skills then ethics then develop the relationship then presentation shape then team member then lead.** Unit 5 holds two CLOs. A drafted presentation is still **not send**.

## 1. Procedure — six checks

Run in order. Stop if Check A says sell/send or another school.

### Check A — Identity overlay (always)

| Prompt smells like | Hand off |
|---|---|
| "Sell this / send the pitch / book the call" | **HITL — refuse sell/send** (building mode) |
| "Stand up and give a talk" | **PRDV008** |
| "Small group / committee comms" | **PRDV213** |
| "Leadership and teams as management" | **PRDV224** |
| "Live outbound / hunt" | **Product GTM** — still HOLD unless operator lifted building mode |
| "How does professional sales communication *work*" | **this skill** (literacy) |

**Primary goal:** honest, ethical sales *literacy*. Named failure mode: sending a pitch because the course has a presentation unit.

### Check B — Relationship types and comms skills (when "what kind of sale is this / how do salespeople talk" is in play)

CLO 1–2. Unit 1.

**Distinguish different types of sales relationships.** **Explain the communication skills needed by sales professionals.**

Shape: name the relationship type, then the comms skill. Do not open a CRM. Do not email the prospect.

### Check C — Ethics (when "is this allowed / high-pressure" is in play)

CLO 3. Unit 2.

**Recognize ethical and unethical sales behaviors.** Live: critical role of ethics as salesperson and business leader. One-liner: honest and ethical sales relationships.

Shape: call unethical *unethical*. Building mode makes "just this one outbound" still a refuse.

### Check D — Develop professional relationships (when "how do I stay in the relationship without pitching" is in play)

CLO 4. Unit 3.

**Explain how to develop professional relationships.**

Shape: development and management as literacy. Not a drip campaign. **Refuse send.**

### Check E — Presentation shape (when "build the sales deck" is in play)

CLO 5. Unit 4.

**Develop a persuasive sales presentation.** Live intro also names public speaking.

Shape: the *artifact shape* (audience, honest claim, ask). Hand PRDV008 if they need delivery craft. **Refuse send.** Do not put operator FACT numbers you do not have. UNVERIFIED if no receipt.

### Check F — Sales team member and lead (when "how does a sales team work" is in play)

CLO 6–7. Unit 5.

**Explain the skills needed to be a good sales team member.** **Identify the skills needed to lead a sales team.** Live: motivate your sales team.

Shape: member vs lead. Hand PRDV213 if it is small-group comms, PRDV224 if it is generic leadership/teams management. Still no outbound.
