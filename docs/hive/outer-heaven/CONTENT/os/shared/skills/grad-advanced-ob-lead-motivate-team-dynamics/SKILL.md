Run in order. Skip a check only if the user already constrained the frame.

**Level check:** if the prompt is actually undergrad homework in BUS209's six units, **hand off BUS209** and recap only. If it is MMGT604's proctored 80% exam, **refuse exam reconstruction**; this skill is the *content* overlay, not the degree exam.

### Check B — Leadership styles that influence and motivate (when "which style / how do I lead this team / influence" is in play)

CLO 5. Unit 1.

Leadership is **Unit 1**, not the last chapter. Differentiate styles by whether they **influence and motivate** individuals, teams, *and* organizations — three targets.

Saylor OB ch.12 overlay (summarize; confirm live names): trait, behavioral, contingency, contemporary. Encode the *job* (who must be influenced, at which level, toward which outcome in Check A). Do not dump cases.

**Hand off BUS401** for lead-vs-manage as a course. **Hand off BUS208** for POLC. Stay here for "this style, these three targets, this advantage overlay."

### Check C — Contrast motivation theories for a business outcome (when "why won't they move / which theory / incentives vs needs" is in play)

CLO 1. Unit 2.

**Contrast** theories as they relate to OB **and their application to successful business outcomes**. Contrast + application, not a poster of names.

Public-school split (confirm live labels): need-based vs process-based; then job design / goals / appraisal / incentives as application. Do **not** freeze Maslow/Herzberg/Vroom as *the* Saylor list until login.

**Hand off BUS209** for "identify effects of motivation" as intro. **Hand off BUS603** if HRM sits next to the motivation answer. Stay here for "contrast these theories so that outcome moves."

### Check D — Conflict at three levels (when "this fight / this friction / escalate or not" is in play)

CLO 2. Unit 3.

Name the **level**: individual, group, or organizational. The CLO is *examine challenges* at all three.

This is conflict **inside advanced OB**, not the ALS negotiations course. Negotiation may appear as a tool. Do not turn the answer into BUS641.

Saylor OB ch.10 overlay (summarize): type, causes/outcomes, management. Encode the *job* (which level, what the unmanaged friction costs Check A's advantage).

**Hand off BUS641 / BUS403** for deal design as a course. Stay here for "this conflict, these three levels."

### Check E — Contemporary teams at geo scale (when "this team / remote / cross-border / why teams fail now" is in play)

CLO 3. Unit 4.

**Critique** contemporary team issues at **local, state, national, and international** levels. Geo scale is live language. Verb is *critique*, not "define a team."

Live course **splits** this from Group Dynamics (Check F). Unit 4 is the *team as a contemporary object*. Unit 5 is the *person inside the group*.

**Hand off BUS603 Unit 6** if the prompt is managing groups with HRM/culture as the people-manager's course. Stay here for "critique this contemporary team issue at this geo scale."

### Check F — How the individual contributes to group dynamics (when "this person in this group / quiet member / dominating voice" is in play)

CLO 4. Unit 5.

**Distinguish how individuals contribute** to group dynamics. The unit is dynamics; the CLO is the contribution.

Live intro: the individual, the individual within teams, corporate culture. Culture is named, not isolated (**BUS603** / **BUS209** own culture-as-a-unit).

Keep the split with Check E: do not answer a "what's wrong with this team as a contemporary form" prompt using only personality, and do not answer a "what is this person doing to the group" prompt using only geo-scale team design.

**Hand off BUS209** for intro group identity/communication. Stay here for "this person's contribution to this group's dynamics."
