# Leadership and teams: power, quality leadership, stages, conflict (clustered)

**Use when:** a prompt needs **leadership and teams** — manager working with and through other people; motivation overlay; leader-must-be-manager; leadership skills, power, and politics; quality leadership; stages of team development and managing-team dynamics, including productive professional conflict — in the Saylor PRDV224 **Leadership and Teams** school (9 h). **Never send.**

**Do not use when:** reconstructing exams; **PRDV213** small-group *communication*; **PRDV217** sales-team member/lead; **PRDV221** why-managers-exist; **PRDV230** workplace conflict *policy*; **BUS641** strategic negotiations; inventing Tuckman (or any stage-model name); treating the reused late-1800s **description** as this course’s job; promising ACE; **sending**.

## 1. Procedure — six checks

Run in order. Stop if Check A says another school or send.

### Check A — Identity overlay (always)

| Prompt smells like | Hand off |
|---|---|
| "Small group comms / committee talk" | **PRDV213** |
| "Lead a *sales* team" | **PRDV217** (still no sell) |
| "Why do managers exist" | **PRDV221** |
| "Write a conflict-resolution *policy*" | **PRDV230** |
| "BATNA / strategic negotiations" | **BUS641** |
| "Lead this team — power, stages, conflict" | **this skill** |
| "Send this" | **HITL — refuse send** |

**Primary goal:** a real team (complementary skills, common purpose) led without turning conflict personal. Named failure mode: calling every group a team, inventing Tuckman, or sending.

### Check B — Management vs leadership, quality leadership (when "leader or manager" is in play)

CLO 2. Unit 1.

**Apply the essential principles of quality leadership.** Live: a good leader must be a good manager to get a lot accomplished. Motivation overlay: persuade and inspire others to commit.

Shape: leadership principles, still through other people. Do not dump an invented leadership-model roster.

### Check C — Power, influence, politics (when "how do I actually move people" is in play)

CLO 1. Unit 2.

**Analyze the key elements of leadership skills, power, and politics.** Live unit: Management's Power and Influence.

Shape: skills + power + politics as the live triad. Not a manipulation how-to. Not 223 ethics-as-course unless the prompt is "is this right."

### Check D — What a team is (when "is this even a team" is in play)

CLO 3 overlay. Units 3–4.

**Discuss the stages of team development and the dynamics of managing teams** starts with knowing it is a team. Live: complementary skills; common purpose (task, problem, internal process); term is often diluted; distributed / international membership.

Shape: name purpose and complementary skills first. If it is really small-group *talk*, hand 213.

### Check E — Manage, build, stages (when "how do I run this team" is in play)

CLO 3. Units 5–6.

**Discuss the stages of team development and the dynamics of managing teams.** Live: manager establishes goals and selects members; team works more or less independently under a leader who organizes and manages. Units: team management; team-building.

Shape: stages as a job, **not a branded model**. Do not name Tuckman. Dynamics = select, purpose, supervise without smothering.

### Check F — Team conflict (when "this team is fighting" is in play)

Unit 7. Intro overlay on CLO 3 dynamics.

Live: best teams experience conflicts; productive force for new ideas and options; key is avoiding professional conflict spilling into personal relationships.

Shape: keep it professional and useful. Hand 213 if it is group-comms techniques, 212 if it is a dyad, 230 if they want a *policy*. **Refuse send.**
