# Human communication survey: process, verbal/nonverbal, ethics, theory (clustered)

**Use when:** a prompt needs the **foundations survey** — process, verbal and written, nonverbal, perception, ethics in a diverse society, culture on interpersonal *and* group, apply theories, accurate language, ethical vs unethical — in the Saylor PRDV211 **Foundations of Human Communication** school (20 h). Critical consumer *and* producer of messages (message, audience, medium, cultural context). **Never send.**

**Do not use when:** reconstructing exams; **PRDV008** presentations; **PRDV104** workplace etiquette; **PRDV212** interpersonal as a *course*; **PRDV213** group; **PRDV214** org; **PRDV215** mass; **PRDV002** professional writing; **ENGL***; promising ACE; **sending** the message.

## 1. Procedure — six checks

Run in order. Stop if Check A says another school or send.

### Check A — Identity overlay (always)

| Prompt smells like | Hand off |
|---|---|
| "Stand up and give the talk" | **PRDV008** |
| "Workplace manners / Slack tone" | **PRDV104** |
| "Interpersonal / group / org / mass as a *course*" | **PRDV212–215** |
| "Write the email / report" | **PRDV002** |
| "Essay / composition" | **ENGL*** (leave) |
| "How does human communication work as a survey" | **this skill** |
| "Send this" | **HITL — refuse send** |

**Primary goal:** a critical consumer and producer of messages in context. Named failure mode: jumping to a specialized course or sending.

### Check B — Process and study (when "what even is communication" is in play)

CLO 1. Unit 1.

**Identify the major components of the human communication process.** Live: transaction between individuals; forms; message, audience, medium, cultural context.

Shape: name the components before picking a channel. Do not skip to slides (PRDV008) or etiquette (PRDV104).

### Check C — Verbal and written language (when "how do I say/write this" is in play)

CLO 2, 3, 9. Unit 2.

**Describe the significance of written and verbal communication in various contexts.** **Explain the key characteristics and elements of verbal communication.** **Use language accurately, expressively, and appropriately in various scenarios.**

Shape: context first, then verbal/written characteristics, then accurate/expressive/appropriate language. Hand PRDV002 if they need a *document*. Hand PRDV008 if they need to *deliver a talk*. Stay here for the survey of verbal.

### Check D — Nonverbal and roles (when "what they saw vs what I said" is in play)

CLO 4. Unit 3.

**Describe the roles of verbal and nonverbal communication.** Live unit is nonverbal; CLO pairs both roles.

Shape: what verbal is doing vs what nonverbal is doing in *this* setting. Do not turn this into PRDV104 etiquette of the room unless manners/ethics-of-courtesy is the job.

### Check E — Perception, ethics, culture (when "they heard it differently / is this ethical" is in play)

CLO 5, 6, 7, 10. Unit 4.

**Explain the perception of self and others.** **Describe the ethical obligations of communicators within a diverse society.** **Describe the impact of diversity and culture on interpersonal communication and group communication.** **Identify ethical and unethical applications of communication.**

Shape: perception first, then ethics in a diverse society, then culture on interpersonal *and* group as survey (not 212/213). Call unethical applications *unethical* when the prompt is that. Do not write SOC101. Do not send.

### Check F — Apply theories (when "which theory / why did this land" is in play)

CLO 8. Unit 5.

**Apply communication theories across different settings.** Live intro: principles and theories used to assess and explain practices.

Shape: setting first, then a theory *as assessment*, not a name-drop dump. Guest pages do not list the theory roster — do not invent one.

## 2. Worked patterns (shape only)

**"Why did that Slack land cold even though the words were fine?"**
Check D (verbal vs nonverbal/virtual cues) then E (perception). Etiquette overlay is PRDV104 if it is manners, not process.

**"Is this message ethical to send to the whole org?"**
Check E (ethical vs unethical; diverse society). Then F if they want theory. **Refuse send.**

**"Give me interpersonal communication as a course."**
Check A → PRDV212. Recap CLO 7 here only as culture/diversity *impact*.

**"I need a speech."**
Check A → PRDV008. Recap C here only if they need verbal-language accuracy first.
