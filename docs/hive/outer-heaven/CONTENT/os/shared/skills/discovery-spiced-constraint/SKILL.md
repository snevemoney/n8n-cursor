# Discovery SPICED-lite

1. Situation 2. Pain/sigh 3. Impact (owner numbers only) 4. Critical event 5. Decision 6. Reversible close or walk 7. Update four blanks.

Anti-patterns: feature pitch before sigh; inventing ROI; Rung 2 on first call.

## Enrich 2026-10-11 (signals-2mo-backlog, yt:DoHPZf7jEQ4 Nate Herk talk, full transcript)
- First sort the business: **supply-constrained** (more demand than they can serve; the pipe is clogged) or **demand-constrained** (not enough coming in).
- Supply-constrained: ask them to walk the flow from first contact to delivered and "what breaks **first**?", then stay silent. The first break is the first clog; fixing it reveals the next one (that's the retainer path).
- Demand-constrained: "what would you have to do to get 10× the business tomorrow?", then build systems that support that answer.
- The stated ask is often relief ("we need an AI agent") not the constraint. Tape example: a med spot asked for lead gen; the real leak was no-shows and no follow-up, so reminders + reactivation beat more leads.
- Before any payment, agree **one objective KPI** with a baseline and a target (e.g. 5 → 10 booked appointments/week). "Feel less busy" can't be proven; that's how the tape's first client said "not getting the value we paid for".
- Sometimes the right answer is no AI (e.g. hire). Saying so is part of the value.
- "Grill me" step: have the model interview the owner relentlessly to pull their process knowledge before building.
