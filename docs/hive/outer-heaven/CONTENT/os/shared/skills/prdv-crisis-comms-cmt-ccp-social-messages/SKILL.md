# Crisis comms: CMT, CCP, social plan, messages — never send (clustered)

**Use when:** a prompt needs **crisis communication as PR** — protect and defend brand/reputation in a sudden event: what/why, anticipate, CMT, social *plan*, CCP design/test, key messages — in the Saylor PRDV107 **Crisis Communication** school (20 h). **Never send. Never post.**

**Do not use when:** reconstructing exams; **PRDV104** everyday etiquette; **PRDV008** presentations; **PRDV211–215** comms surveys; hive **Watchdog** ops incidents; promising ACE; **posting** to Twitter/Facebook/site; **sending** a news release; treating a worked example as a live operator statement.

This is **one** skill. Five live units, six CLOs. Loop: **what/why → anticipate + CMT → social plan → CCP design/test → key messages.** CMT is a CLO, not a sixth unit.

## 0. Hygiene

- Label **school**. Saylor **PRDV107**. LMS **749**. Catalog **20 h / 2 CEU**. ACE **N**. Certificate: only the final counts; **70%**; live syllabus **14-day** wait; **max 3** (like PRDV008–011, **not** 7-day unlimited on 102–104). Practice exam + unit assessments do not factor. 2.0 CEUs on the certificate. Not a degree twin. Not Watchdog.
- Label **claim vs data**. Guest only. Units never opened. No intro-video activity. Guest objects: syllabus `67693`, AI zone `102908`. Syllabus last edited **2025-09-17**. Indexed Unit 4 blurb is parallel, not guest-expanded. **No LMS Chrome** until BUS501. **No cert final.**
- **CMT wording collision:** live intro = **crisis communication team (CMT)**; CLO 3 = **crisis management team (CMT)**; Unit 4 blurb = **CCT** plus a roster. Encode the collision. Do not silently pick one expansion.
- Four live categories of crisis: **acts of God, mechanical problems, human error, management decision or indecision.** Examples in intro are illustrations, not an exam list.
- Crisis comms ≠ etiquette ≠ presentation ≠ hive incident. Specialized PR function: protect and defend brand/reputation. **Never send/post.** Communications Manager drafts; HITL before any public statement. Do not update a live website from this skill.
- Six **live** CLOs. Do not invent ACE / SAYA.

## 1. Procedure — six checks

Run in order. Stop if Check A says send/post or a different school.

### Check A — Identity overlay (always)

| Prompt smells like | Hand off |
|---|---|
| "Workplace manners / Slack tone / inclusion as etiquette" | **PRDV104** |
| "Stand up and give the talk" | **PRDV008** |
| "Comms as a survey course" | **PRDV211–215** |
| "Hive is down / watchdog / prod" | **Watchdog** (ops — not this PR school) |
| "How do we protect brand in a sudden event — plan, team, messages" | **this skill** |
| "Post this / send the release / tweet it" | **HITL — refuse send** |

**Primary goal:** a credible, tested communication plan so a CMT/CCT can respond. Named failure mode: posting, inventing facts, or treating the quarterly-drill vignette as operator FACT.

### Check B — What and why (when "is this even a crisis / why comms" is in play)

CLO 1. Unit 1.

**Explain what crisis communication involves and why it is important.** Live: sudden unexpected serious event; protect and defend brand/reputation; PR specialist helps management communicate sound, safe, responsible actions; who/what/when/where facts; internal vs external stakeholders.

Shape: name the category (four live types), name stakeholders, name the job (protect/defend with facts). Do not issue a statement.

### Check C — Anticipate and CMT (when "who is on the team / we should have drilled" is in play)

CLO 2–3. Unit 2 (+ intro CMT).

**Explain how to anticipate and make advanced preparations for a crisis.** **Describe the role of a crisis management team (CMT).** Live intro: quarterly disaster preparedness exercise; know what to do, who to contact, alternate office; your role on the team.

Shape: prepare *before* the morning fire vignette. Roster, roles, contacts, drills. Keep the CMT vs CCT vs "communication team (CMT)" labels visible. Do not page real people from this skill.

### Check D — Social media *plan* (when "should we tweet" is in play)

CLO 4. Unit 3.

**Understand how to create an effective social media plan for crisis situations.** Live intro names Twitter and Facebook as channels to inform stakeholders with an appropriate key message. Live also stresses social in the CCP *and in marketing*.

Shape: a **plan** (who posts, what is factual, which channel, internal vs external). **Refuse the post.** A drafted tweet is not delivery. HITL if they still want it out. Do not use this as a marketing calendar.

### Check E — CCP design, prepare, test (when "write the plan" is in play)

CLO 5. Unit 4.

**Explain how to design, prepare, and test a crisis communication plan (CCP).** Indexed blurb: no one-size-fits-all; pre-crisis / crisis / post-crisis; safety, notifications, CCT roster/functions/contacts, situation assessment, key messages, media releases, updates; post-crisis evaluate and loop; test/practice; coordinate among participating orgs; spokesperson; sample releases; media training; length depends on org type/size.

Shape: the *plan* artifact, then **test**. A CCP is not a send. Do not fill it with invented operator phone numbers.

### Check F — Key messages (when "what do we say / to whom" is in play)

CLO 6. Unit 5.

**Practice crafting key messages to a target audience.** Live: appropriate key message to internal (senior leadership, employees, investors) and external (customers, clients, local officials, public); who/what/when/where facts.

Shape: audience first, then a factual message. **Refuse send.** Comms Manager if they want a draft in the operator's voice. HITL before it leaves the building. Do not invent casualties, causes, or legal admissions.

## 2. Worked patterns (shape only)

**"The building is on fire. Tweet now."**
Check A then D: social is a *plan*, not a post. Then F for the message shape. **Refuse tweet.** HITL.

**"We have no plan and no team."**
Check C then E. Anticipate + CMT + CCP. Drill/test before the next event. Do not page anyone.

**"Is a CEO resignation a crisis?"**
Check B. Live examples include CEOs asked to resign after inappropriate behavior. Category: management decision/indecision (or human error — do not over-fit). Then F for messages if they need words. Still no send.

**"Write the CCP and the holding statement and send both."**
Check E then F then A: **refuse send**. Draft ≠ delivery.

**"Hive n8n is down, post status."**
Check A → Watchdog. This school is PR brand-protection, not hive ops.
