### Check B — Units and measurement (when "SI / convert / sig figs" is in play)

CLO 1. Unit 1.

**Common units and S.I. prefixes; significant digits; converting units.**

Shape: name the unit, then convert or round. Do not merge with CHEM101; this is the mechanics overlay.

### Check C — Linear kinematics (when "1D motion / v and a" is in play)

CLO 2. Unit 2.

**Kinematic equations for one-dimensional linear-motion problems.**

Shape: 1D motion as a job. **Equation names/forms are not guest-titled.** Do not mint \(v=v_0+at\) as a Saylor title. If the prompt already uses those forms, treat as this CLO.

### Check D — 2D and projectiles (when "projectile / two dimensions" is in play)

CLO 3. Unit 3.

**Kinematic equations for two-dimensional problems such as projectile motion.**

Shape: 2D then projectile. **Literacy only.** Refuse aiming/weapons how-to. Same color rule as MA008 rocket-angle.

### Check E — Dynamics and gravity (when "force / Newton's laws / weight" is in play)

CLO 4. Unit 4.

**Newton's laws of motion for dynamic problems involving various types of forces such as the universal law of gravity.** Newton's laws live as a phrase. Universal law of gravity live. **F=ma is not a guest title.**

Shape: identify the force family, then Newton's laws. Do not invent friction/normal/tension names as Saylor-titled (CLO says “various types”).

### Check F — Circular and rotational (when "circle / spin / torque-ish" is in play)

CLOs 5–6. Units 5–6.

**Centripetal force and rotational kinematics for circular motion. Statics and dynamic problems involving rotation.**

Shape: circular then rotation. Torque is **not** a live CLO word — do not mint it as a Saylor title. If the prompt uses torque, treat as rotational statics/dynamics.

### Check G — Energy and collisions (when "work / KE / crash" is in play)

CLOs 7–8. Units 7–8.

**Work-energy theorem and conservation of energy to analyze machines and to solve linear and rotational problems. Conservation of momentum for elastic and inelastic collisions (linear and rotational).**

Shape: work-energy then momentum/collisions. **Machines** are live as an analysis job, not a build. **Collisions are literacy, not crash-test / weapons protocols.** Elastic and inelastic both live.
