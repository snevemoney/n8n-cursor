## Structure vs control card 2026-09-01 (`yt:W_MjBT-7Hb4`) FACT-as-stated (BizDoc OpenAI case)

When the packet is a named company, add a **structure card**. Ownership ≠ control.

1. Legal wrapper (nonprofit / capped-profit / sub / AG consent) vs who sits on the board.
2. Cap table vs voting/control rights (tape analog: kids inherit 25% each, parents still decide).
3. Commitments vs run-rate (infra/capex pledges vs revenue). Tape $ = FACT-as-stated — re-check live, never quote as ours.
4. Early vs right: being first can create mess during a charter reset; being later can look cleaner. Note both. Do not pick a winner for the operator's money.
5. Structure can be a strategy — record it. Do not advise securities.

No outreach. Save the card in the company packet (chat-only = not done).
