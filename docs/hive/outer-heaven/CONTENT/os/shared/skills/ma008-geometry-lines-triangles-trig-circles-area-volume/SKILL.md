**Primary goal:** describe and measure 2D/3D figures with the live CLO set. Named failure mode: dumping MA007 algebra, inventing SOHCAHTOA, or encoding the rocket sentence as aiming instructions.

### Check B — Basics and angle facts (when "point / ray / complementary" is in play)

CLOs 1, 8, 10. Unit 1 overlay. Unit 9/10 description overlay.

**Undefined notions: point, line, distance along a line, distance around a circular arc.** Identify angles, rays, line segments, points. Supplementary, complementary, vertical, adjacent for unknown angles. Describe objects using geometric shapes, measures, properties.

Shape: name the object, then the angle fact. Do not invent Euclid's postulates as live names.

### Check C — Parallel lines (when "cut by a transversal / corresponding angles" is in play)

CLO 3. Unit 2.

**Calculate angles created by parallel lines.**

Shape: parallel-line angles as a job. Transversal / corresponding / alternate names are **not** in the guest CLO list — do not mint them as live. If the prompt uses those words, still solve as parallel-line angles without claiming Saylor titled them.

### Check D — Congruence, triangles, polygons (when "same shape and size / triangle facts" is in play)

CLOs 2. Units 3–5.

**Determine if figures are congruent.** Units 3–4 are triangle overlays (congruence and other/triangle relationships) without extra CLO names. Unit 5 polygons and quadrilaterals overlay.

Shape: congruence as a job. Do not invent SSS/SAS/ASA/HL as live unless a later harvest titles them. Guest did not.

### Check E — Similarity (when "same shape, different size" is in play)

CLO 4. Unit 6.

**Determine if two given figures are similar.**

Shape: similarity as a job. Scale-factor name is not in the guest CLO — do not invent it as live.

### Check F — Right triangles and circles (when "Pythagorean / trig / inscribed" is in play)

CLOs 5, 6, 9. Units 7–8.

**Trigonometric ratios and the Pythagorean theorem to solve right triangles.** Circles: inscribed angles, radii, chords; area and circumference formulas.

Shape: right triangle then circle. **Pythagorean theorem is live by name.** **Trigonometric ratios is live as a phrase; ratio names are not listed.** Do not encode SOHCAHTOA / sine / cosine / tangent as Saylor-titled. If the prompt already uses sine, treat it as the live “trigonometric ratios” CLO without claiming the names were on the syllabus.

### Check G — Perimeter, area, surface area, volume (when "how much paint / how much space inside" is in play)

CLOs 7, 9, 11. Units 9–10. Intro: paint a wall; pack a car; seed for land.

**Volume of cylinders, pyramids, cones, and spheres.** Area and circumference of a circle. Area, volume, and surface area of composites of triangles, quadrilaterals, polygons, cubes, and right prisms. Perimeter is Unit 9 title overlay.

Shape: 2D measure then 3D measure. Paint/packing examples are literacy, not a send and not a procurement order.
