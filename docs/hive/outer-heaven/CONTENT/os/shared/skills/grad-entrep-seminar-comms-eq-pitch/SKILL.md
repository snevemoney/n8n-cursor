# Graduate seminar: comms, EQ, negotiate, pitch (clustered)

**Use when:** a founder needs **running-the-business skills** after launch — communication methods/modalities, personal skills (stress/attitudes), emotional-intelligence gaps, business skills (negotiation, decision-making, networking), and analysis of business plans and pitches for stakeholder desirability — in Saylor BUS654 (**taken last** after BUS650–653).

**Do not use when:** BUS653 launch ops; BUS652 ideation; BUS651 law; BUS650 leadership; BUS305 undergrad; MEI610 exam; ACE promises; sending a pitch; spend/money movement; exam reconstruction.

This is **one** skill. Four units = **comms → personal/EQ → business skills → selling the idea.** Five CLOs (EI is its own — do not force 1:1). **Analyze a pitch; do not send it.**

## 1. Procedure — six checks

### Check A — Identity

| Prompt smells like | Hand off |
|---|---|
| Launch / grow operations | **BUS653** |
| Generate / vet / pick an idea | **BUS652** |
| Entity / contracts / life cycle | **BUS651** |
| POLC / entrepreneurial leadership | **BUS650** |
| Undergrad foundations | **BUS305** |
| Sit exam / send a pitch / spend | **refuse** |
| Founder comms, EQ, negotiation, plan+pitch critique | **this skill** |

### Check B — Comms (CLO 1; Unit 1)

Choose appropriate communication methods and modalities as an entrepreneur. Presentations + technology and vision/mission communication are intro-named. Not BUS653 strategy-as-the-course.

### Check C — Personal skills (CLO 2; Unit 2)

Develop personal skills such as stress management and work attitudes. Time and personality are intro-named.

### Check D — EQ (CLO 3; Unit 2)

Evaluate emotional intelligence skills and address gaps. **Own CLO** — do not fold into C. Same unit as personal skills; keep the CLO distinct.

### Check E — Business skills (CLO 4; Unit 3)

Negotiation, decision-making, networking. Teams, exit strategy, projects, and business challenges are intro-named. Not legal advice (BUS651). Not BUS650 POLC-as-the-course.

### Check F — Plan + pitch (CLO 5; Unit 4)

Analyze business plans and pitches to ensure they are desirable to stakeholders. Recalibrate the plan and story/pitch are intro-named. **Analyze, do not send.** HITL before any send/pay.

## 2. Worked patterns (shape only)

**I need investor-ready.** F is analysis. HITL before any send/pay. Not BUS653 financing-as-growth-method. Not a spend card.

**BUS654 or BUS653?** Founder comms / EQ / pitch critique after 650–653 → this skill. Launch/grow operations → BUS653.
