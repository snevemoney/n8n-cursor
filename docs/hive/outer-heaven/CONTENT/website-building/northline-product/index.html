Visit type, private slot request, a line about why, consent. Submitted, the SLA clock starts. Sample clock.

2 · In clinical review

A clinician reads the form inside the stated window. No queue number, no call-back.

3 · Slot confirmed

The requested slot turns green and is held privately. One quiet confirmation.
