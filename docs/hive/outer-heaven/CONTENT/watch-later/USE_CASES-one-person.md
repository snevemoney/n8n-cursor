## 1. Review → book (dental)

**On tape:** Dental clinics get Google reviews and do not turn a good review into another appointment.  
Workflow: monitor new reviews → thank-you + **booking link** → 5-star follow-up asking for referrals.  
Claim: 15 booked appointments/month · $2k/mo/clinic · 10 clinics (UNVERIFIED).

**Steal as:** `review-to-book`  
**Path:** A (named clinic)  
**Hive:** `lead-web-find` (dentist / local pro with reviews + weak book) → MUST → `constraint-position` → `four-blank-sku` → margin → `warm-draft-hitl` → `private-book-install` (thank-you + book link, HITL send) → `proof-30-60-90`  
**KPI:** new 4–5★ reviews that get a book link · booked appointments from that path  
**Not:** mass-DM, fake reviews, auto-send without HITL.

Closest old row: `Review/rebook` was GROW after a win. This one is **ACQUIRE from public reviews**.

## 2. Long episode → shorts factory (Sarah)

**On tape:** Podcast editor. Long ep → 30 high-retention shorts/week. $3k/mo/client · 6 clients · 2h/client/week · 45 min review (UNVERIFIED).  
Loop: upload → transcript+timestamps → pick 10 clips (hooks/punchlines/story) → cut → captions + thumbs + platform copy → **human reviews and ships**.

**Steal as:** `clip-factory`  
**Path:** C for *our* Publishing Engine · A if selling to a named podcaster/creator  
**Hive:** Creative Studio + Publishing Engine. Cursor cuts/scripts; Grok writes hooks/descriptions. Evens ships.  
**On tape he named Opus/Descript/custom GPT — we do not install those.** Same stages on Cursor + Grok.  
**KPI:** clips shipped / week · time per episode  
**Not:** “AI content agency” as the offer. Outcome = N shorts/week from their long form.

## 3. Person B landing brief (exec coaches)

**On tape:** Person A: “write a landing page for my coaching business” → generic, quits.

**Steal as:** `orchestrated-site-brief`  
**Path:** C (our/client page)

**Not:** one-shot “make me a coaching site.”

## 4. Same-day positioning + content plan

**On tape:** Client: positioning + content plan for a launch in 2 weeks.  
10-person agency: 7 days, kickoff → research → internal review → present, $8k.  
One-person: 2 hours research + 3 options + calendars, refine, deliver same/next day, $3k (UNVERIFIED).

**Steal as:** `speed-positioning`  
**Path:** A (named client already paying for strategy)  
**Hive:** Researcher packet (competitors) → Consultant 3 options → GTM one calendar → Loom.  
**KPI:** hours to first draft · client picks one option  
**Not:** a 7-day agency theater. Not a thin “AI strategy” PDF with no ICP.

**On tape:** Upwork/Fiverr, jobs **>$500**, sort by completed jobs = validated demand. Then: can our stack do ~80%? Test on self or one pilot. Proof (Loom/screenshot/testimonial). One channel deep.

**Steal as:** `demand-validate`  
**Path:** B / Researcher before a new SKU

## Kill list (do not sell)

He says these collapse when output is cheap:

- Basic content packages  
- Generic landing pages  
- Repetitive admin / copy-paste formatting  
- “I do marketing with AI” / “I offer AI services”

Matches hive: no chatbot-commodity, no vanity redesign, no “I do AI.”

## What he says still sells

Direction · distribution · taste · trust · positioning · a numbered outcome for a specific group.
