## D. Procedures
1. Hit publish (HITL).
2. Audit title: score click + SEO; pick a both-rewrite; keyword near front, hook in the rest.
3. Transcript → description; money/contact links above the fold; SEO under.
4. Timestamped transcript → tease chapters; uncheck auto; plan an A/B.
5. Tags into upload defaults once (~30s).
6. Pin comment (conversation + link) at go-live.
7. Community/post tab + thumbnail the second it is live.
8. First hour: reply here and on other videos; screenshot → human reply; keep threads going.
9. Three clip suggestions → human cut → other platforms.
10. Cover-test thumbnail (≤4 words, not the title, specific-number gap).
11. End screen names the next video every time.

Questions: Does this title do both jobs? Can I cover the title and still know? Did timestamps help or hurt last time?

Signals: first-hour watches; red-dot return traffic; comment threads that go 3–5 deep.
