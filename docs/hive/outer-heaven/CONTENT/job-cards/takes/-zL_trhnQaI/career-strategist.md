## C. Mental Models
Tool ≠ career. Phase-glued builders race to the bottom. Doctor names the disease; pharmacist fills the script. Two roads, one loop. Label expires; edge does not. Constraint before clever. KPI before build. Proof before title. “What have you built?” beats “I use Claude.” Do not quit / do not brand as the prerequisite. Owner’s first client is the owner.

## D. Procedures
1. Audit the current role with a constraint lens (what gates revenue / slows the team / bleeds downstream) — not a repetitive-task dump.
2. For each item, write the number you would move.
3. Pick one. Scope and build. Ask to implement in one corner only.
4. Treat the result as the first case study (boss / team / portfolio / LinkedIn — send is HITL).
5. Repeat until patterns show (same handful of problems).
6. Formalize: take evidence to leadership and propose the role — or be the first name when they are ready.
7. Put the track record somewhere sendable (even a personal site).

Questions he implies: What is actually constraining us? What number moves if we fix it? Why you over the tutorial twin?

Signals: coworkers start coming to you; recruiter asks “what have you built?”
