## D. Procedures

1. **Refuse the phase brief.** “Start an agency / sell automations / become CAIO” is not a job until a constraint is named.
2. **Pick a road from temperament**, not from a title slide. Independent = sales + variety. In-house / `us` = one place, depth. Hive default is `us` first.
3. **Audit with a constraint lens.** Walk the role / the business. Not “what is repetitive.” What slows the team, gates revenue, or bleeds.

5. **One corner.** Scope and implement in one area. Produce a case study you can show (boss, team, sendable page). Not a company-wide roll-out.
6. **Repeat until patterns appear.** Same handful of problems. Now you can diagnose, not just build what you are handed.
7. **Formalize only with evidence.** Propose a seat / a retainer / a desk from the inside. Triangle before any new hive row.
8. **Keep proof sendable.** Links, not a tutorial résumé. Building in public is optional; a quiet portfolio is enough.
9. **Caveat every tape stat.** $ / job-loss % / CAIO % stay UNVERIFIED.
