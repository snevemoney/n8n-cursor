## C. Mental Models
No stock, no horse, still have a read. Sincere and convenient can coexist. Treaties fail like contracts when the other side wants the prize more. Chips/power may be visible; incentive is the wall. Anyone with a finished future is selling. Judgment/taste remain when the tool is ubiquitous. Government can yank a SKU (Fable/Mythos suspend) without being the referee they asked for.

## D. Procedures
1. When a lab asks to slow down: quote the incentive sentence; note they will not unilaterally stop.
2. Do not pick a horse. Inventory your repeating work.
3. Pick one loop you already do. Run it through a tool. That is the whole move.
4. Teach one person you care about the same way — existing work, not a new passion.
5. Treat rented intelligence as revocable (price, rules, cutoff, government suspend).
6. Aim at judgment/taste: what is worth doing.

Questions: Are they offering to stop or asking for a referee? What repeating work can I run this week? Signals: IPO + pause letter in the same week. Qualify: you are a seat, not the mission.
