## D. Procedures

1. **Name the end-state:** lifestyle / employed-AI / services firm / product. Commit. Do not hop this week.
2. **Find distribution or a warm buyer** (his: YouTube). No buyer → no agency.
3. **Qualify the company:** Is there a written (or writeable) SOP? One KPI with a P&L through-line? Benchmark today?
4. **Same-page workshop** (landscape, assumptions, where this is going). Fixed, small. Checkable stop: they can repeat the assumptions.
5. **Industry possibilities session** after they handed pain. Checkable stop: they treat you as the expert who also understands *them*.
6. **Current-state discovery:** money spent to produce X; data flows; first-principles “rebuild this operation with AI” — not a wishlist of dashboards.
7. **Map to ROI.** If no through-line, stop or shrink. Nate: if the clog is mid-pipe, do not sell more top-of-funnel AI.
8. **Push back.** Offer no-AI syncs if that is the clog. “Do you want this to not work?”
9. **Blueprint** as a portable deliverable (they *may* take it elsewhere). Then optional build.
10. **Success criteria** written by a human who knows the right artifact. Volume thought-experiment (100 vs 10,000).
11. **PM looks at the payload**, not only the output. Missing fields = fail, not “hallucination.”
12. **Partnership / performance** only after the KPI is locked and the system is proven. Else project or stop.
13. **Package** the method as named steps. Refuse “build whatever.”
14. **Hire** other talents when the offer needs them — not 15 copies of the founder, not a nameless agent army.

**Qualify / frame:** This is an agency-exit podcast, not a hive SKU. Custom AI Studio is his firm. JBL/e-com are *his* clients, not ours.
**Objections:** “We need 12 agents” → show the unused-second-brain and the P&L question. “$100M” → clickbait + they have not sold. “Just build what I wrote” → scope-creep / unused early projects.

## F. Decision Rules

- If no SOP and no KPI → do not build; discover or walk.
- If the request is a chatbot / second brain / 12-agent PDF → push back; find the workflow to collapse.
- If the clog is mid-pipe → do not sell more top-of-funnel AI (Nate).
- If you cannot explain success without the model → the checklist is not done.
- If error is 1% at 100 → compute it at production volume before ship.
- If they want partnership → require P&L through-line + attribution; else project or no.
- If you are talking RAG on a sales call → stop; talk relief and unique mechanism.
- If you would “just do whatever they want” → you are in the first-20-projects trap.
- If the idea is a product in a 60-day-obsolete stack → services first (his path).
- If you have not committed lifestyle vs exit → do not hire an agency narrative.
