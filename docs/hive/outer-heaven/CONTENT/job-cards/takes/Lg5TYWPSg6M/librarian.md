## C. Mental Models
Cost ≠ price. Hours then errors then revenue. Whether vs which. Scope-creep is a v2 signal. Utility bill in their name.

## D. Procedures
1. Do not say a number until they say the cost of the problem.
2. LRP; why this / now / me; walk if they only want a shop-quote.
3. Annualize; pick 10–20%; three tiers; proposal opens with their now/want.
4. 30-day objective milestones; backlog v2; cut scope not price.
5. Client card for run; you eat test; write the volume assumption.
6. Measure after-state at 30/60/90.
Avoid: $20k/100k as FACT; 10× guarantee; hive agency; Skool doc as SSOT.
