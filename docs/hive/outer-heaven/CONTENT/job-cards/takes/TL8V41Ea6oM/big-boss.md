## D. Procedures

1. **Name the pillar** (one C). Do not fuse four jobs.
2. **Write the five parts** (job, tool, buckets, output, boundary).
3. **Connect the minimum tool.** Read the permission dialog. Draft ≠ send. Calendar read ≠ move meetings.
4. **Run until the output is trusted** (days/weeks on email).
5. **Add the next layer** (calendar vs urgent mail).
6. **Only then schedule** the morning run. Sensitive + unattended = extra care.
7. **Creativity:** drop rough notes in a folder; name audience/length/format; force questions on gaps; open the real file; direct edits; optional style-match.
8. **Clarity telescope:** company + emails + news + docs → one picture → verify + concise → optional second engine.
9. **Clarity microscope:** never “summarize”; five-column risk table; human asks the questions.
10. **Coaching:** dump context; stay in character; voice; break character; harder persona; one-page card.
