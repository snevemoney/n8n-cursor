## B. Atomic Knowledge

### Manage is the wrong verb; risk tier does not travel with width
- **Claim:** You set infrastructure, goals, and the last say. Agents escalate. You can widen what they *attempt* without widening what they may *send*.
- **Reasoning:** “Managing agents” sounds like line-managing Susie. She wants to wait for escalations and decide. Host: people use AI *so they don’t have to manage*. Width ≠ permission: she still checks every email.
- **Mechanism:** Goals docs + quarterly review + “do smart things” for invention; human check on the risk tier (email/send).
- **Evidence:** Explicit: “The tier of risk has stayed the same but the width has expanded.”
- **Conditions:** You have written goals and a check on the risky surface.
- **Exceptions:** Trigger automations (video → nine drafts) are narrow and defined; they are not the same as undefined “do smart things.”
- **Action:** Hive already: manage don’t chat; send stays HITL. Steal width-without-risk-creep. Do not steal “do smart things” as unsupervised send.

## D. Procedures

1. **Write goals** (and a north star) before expanding scope.
2. **Feel one agent**, then one proactive agent, then two (route), then a named org + mission control.
3. **Start titles** a founder can say (CMO/CPO). Add only named extras (friction-watcher, ambition-squeezer) after you see handoffs.
4. **Width:** let them propose net-new work against the goals. **Risk:** same gate as before (she still reads every email).
5. **Daily:** dump uncodified judgment into a wiki (dictate). Fix broken pipes (iMessage) instead of hoping the diary covers them.
6. **Watchdog:** dups, calendar conflicts, meeting disagreement, before/after artifacts — with a next action, not a chart.
7. **If building a product:** list primitives you will reuse (login, pay, share, notify). One slice now; factory is the bible for slice two.
8. **Pick bottlenecks by value**, not by fashion (code is often not the bottleneck).
9. **Hard step:** send / pay / deploy stay HITL. “Do smart things” is not a send grant.

**Qualify / frame:** operator-doctrine podcast, not a 34-agent SKU. Fortune 500 / YC / 300k users are her anecdotes.
**Objections:** “Just prompt a workforce in one shot.” Answer: exist ≠ 90%; iteration + watchdog. “Software goes to zero.” Answer: liability, maintenance, lag.
**Avoid:** hundreds of nameless agents; Opus-for-everything; dashboards-as-proof; treating her Slack+Claude as hive OS.
**When to change:** if the same agent is corrected every day, you have an access/context bug (Toby’s job) — do not add more agents.
