## C. Mental Models
Borrow expertise you lack. Disagreement is the feature. Name the missing seat (customer, frontline, beginner). Tailor > generic dump. Skill = repeatable master prompt + template. Subagents report up; teams argue sideways (and cost). Test the method on a topic you already know.

## D. Procedures
1. Scope the topic (questions if vague).
2. Five lenses in parallel.
3. Contradiction map: who has evidence, who is weak.
4. Synthesize into a fixed HTML template.
5. Adversarial verify every citation against the primary source; mark confirmed/corrected/demoted.
6. Name the missing lens; optionally V3.
7. Tailor takeaways to “what do we do differently.”
8. First use: a topic you already know, so you can see the holes.

Questions: Who is missing from the table? Which finding is high-reliability? Signals: only two sources; all five sat in the owner’s chair. Qualify: will this change a decision?
