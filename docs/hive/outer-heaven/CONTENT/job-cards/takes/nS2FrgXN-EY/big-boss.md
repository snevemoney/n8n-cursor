## B. Atomic Knowledge

### The model is never on the phone
- **Claim:** Claude (or any model) runs knowledge, email, talk tracks, lists, and scoring. Humans still dial. That split is the product, not a temporary gap.
- **Reasoning:** Trust is built in the live conversation. AI SDRs and voice agents get hung up on; the tools churn. The opposite error is to throw AI out of outbound entirely.
- **Mechanism:** Five layers around the call. Reps stay the mouth. Approval-to-dial sits behind training modules.
- **Evidence:** Cold open + close. “Not once is it on the phone.”
- **Conditions:** You have humans who will pick up a phone. Marketplace or a single team.
- **Exceptions:** He does not show a world where the model dials “just for inbound.” He rejects that direction.
- **Action:** Auto-dial / AI SDR / voice-to-buyer stays operate-never. Steal the around-the-call stack.

## D. Procedures

1. **Ingest** everything about the offer into one KB (site, PDFs, calls, videos).
2. **Structure** it into the same modules every time (overview → ICP → objections → cases).
3. **Gate:** rep (or desk) is not approved to dial/send until they have gone through that pack.
4. **Draft the playbook as cards** inside a locked framework; assemble; attach top-rep recordings if you have them.
5. **On the block:** talk slow; between calls, draft the email from KB + transcript; send stays HITL here.
6. **Lists:** accounts that match the *buying unit* → 10–15 → spot-check → suppress → cheap enrich → dialer.
7. **After every conversation:** score vs the same rubric the same day; coach on the trend, not a vibe.
8. **If from scratch:** stand up the score loop before the rest of the factory.
9. **Hard step:** dial/send/book stay human. His marketplace CTA is not ours.

**Qualify / frame:** outbound enablement doctrine. Glenn Coco is a marketplace, not a hive SKU. Manufacturing plants are an example buying unit.
**Objections:** “Put Claude on the phone.” Answer: he says that part never changes; AI SDRs churn. “I don’t need coaching, I need more dials.” Answer: he would build scoring first.
**Avoid:** ChatGPT generic script; linear Google Doc as SoT; enriching a thousand dirty rows in the expensive model; quoting 10M dials as FACT.
**When to change:** if the buying unit is not the company name, stop dumping company-level titles.
