## B. Atomic Knowledge

### Roles-based hire is the wrong brief
- **Claim:** “I want to hire an editor” is the old growth unit. That person was going to do four or five things; each thing is a workflow; you need an agent who just does this, and this, and this.
- **Reasoning:** A role hides the work. Workflows make the work drawable. People who will not go granular “will lose.”
- **Mechanism:** Decompose the role into named jobs. One agent per job. Keep updating the prompt the way you’d update an editor.
- **Evidence:** Editor thought-experiment; ~16 activities per role; one at a time.
- **Conditions:** Works when you can name the jobs. Fails when “agent” means a nameless farm.
- **Exceptions:** He still has a full **dev team** and names a duct-tape hire. Humans are not deleted; the brief changes.
- **Action:** Map “one job per agent” to the 17 named desks. Do not spawn a nameless farm because the tape said one agent per thing.
- **Confidence:** high for the belief shift; low that 17 desks must become 120 agents.

## D. Procedures

1. **Name the old role** (“editor,” “videographer”). Do not hire the title yet.
2. **List the four or five jobs** that title was going to do.
3. **Draw the big line** of the business (idea → … → click / dirt). If you cannot draw it, stop.
4. **Explode each word** into six actions until you cannot reduce.
5. **Pick one irreducible slice.** That is the only automate.
6. **Write the SOP as observable behavior** (toddler-level). Refuse vibe words.
7. **Train one agent on that slice.** Update the prompt like you would update a human.
8. **Checkable stop:** the slice has a named owner, a black-and-white done, and a human ship step.
9. **Next slice only after the first one runs.** Do not automate the company.
10. **If a surface answers FAQ** (gym chat): add the capture / book ask — HITL, not auto-book.

**Qualify / frame:** this is a belief-shift tape in a room, not a client delivery. Gym / lawn / fax are props.
**Objections:** “I need an editor” — answer with the four jobs. “AI can’t do it” — answer with training, not magic. “Spawn an agent per action” — answer with 17 named desks.
**Avoid:** nameless farms; auto-voice; rotating hunt to exclusive gym or $150k hire; treating 120 as a headcount.
**When to change:** if the map will not draw, do not automate. If “off brand” is still a feeling, do not automate brand.

## F. Decision Rules

- If the instruction is vibe (“charismatic,” “off brand”) → refuse until black-and-white.
