- **Reasoning:** A role hides a bundle. The bundle is the real org chart.
- **Mechanism:** Draw the business as one linear workflow; go granular until an action cannot be reduced; then automate that atom.
- **Evidence:** “instead of saying I need to hire an editor, you say I need an agent who just does this.”
- **Conditions:** You can name the activities. Cash flow if you hire a human to sit next to teams.
- **Exceptions:** In-person physical work (horses, lawns) is not the now-automation; back office is.
- **Action:** Write the role as a list of observable activities before any hire conversation.

## C. Mental Models
A role is a bundle of workflows. If you cannot draw the business, you do not have one you can automate. Train like a toddler; update like an editor. Vague language is how both humans and machines fail. Reduce until irreducible, then automate. Demand for customers is not PMF. “Off brand” that cannot be described cannot be detected. Humans + better tech beat humans + fax; fax still pays. Opportunity cost of a hire is the un-drawn workflow.

## D. Procedures
1. Draw the business as one linear workflow (idea → … → click / turf).
2. Under each word, list the six actions until you cannot reduce.
3. For a role you were about to hire, list the ~16 activities; pick one.
4. Write the activity in observable behaviors (raise voice / nod / start at highest tension).
5. Train one agent on that one activity; update the prompt when output drifts.
6. If hiring a human, sit them next to teams and have them point at automatable atoms — cash-flow gated.
7. Prioritize: expectation-setting, CAC expectations, brand controllables (black-and-white patterns).

Questions: Can you draw it? What would an editor do first? What does “off brand” mean in describables?

Signals: a chat that answers and never asks; a role request that is actually five workflows.

## F. Decision Rules
- If you cannot draw the workflow, do not hire and do not automate.
- If the instruction is vague, rewrite it to toddler-level before any agent or human.

- If someone says “hire an editor,” ask which of the four or five things.
- Do not quote $150k / $10k gym / 12–24 months as FACT.
