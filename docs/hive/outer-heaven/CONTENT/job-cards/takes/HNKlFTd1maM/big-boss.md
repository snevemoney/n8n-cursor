## D. Procedures

1. **Name the machine** (drafts / quote-HITL / intern / concierge). Do not sum them.
2. **Diagnose clog/leak** in hours or money. Refuse “I can build a chatbot.”
3. **Write the before-number** before any price. No baseline → no case study later.
4. **Draft vs send:** if the artifact is copy, stop at ready-to-plug. Human sends.
5. **Price from value that compounds**, not from complexity or last invoice.
6. **Read the close-rate:** >>50% / no pushback → number is low (his rule). Do not treat as a win.
7. **Seats:** CEO does not deliver; CTO does not connect nodes; account manager owns the client.
8. **Scope lock + change request** for “one more feature.”
9. **QA:** one week internal (sample + real + edge) → then client week. Client does not see v0.
10. **Package only if you have a repeatable offer.** Anchor is a later move.
