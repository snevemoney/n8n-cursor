2. Discovery: hours, $ per hour, errors; write a baseline or you will not have a case study.
3. Do not send from the first outreach system — fill a DB.
4. If close >~50% with no friction → raise.
5. Tiers; lead with the high number.
6. Written include/exclude + change request.
7. 1 week internal QA (sample+real+edge) → 1 week client QA.
8. Do not park the CTO on nodes; do not chase five small retainers for “stability.”
