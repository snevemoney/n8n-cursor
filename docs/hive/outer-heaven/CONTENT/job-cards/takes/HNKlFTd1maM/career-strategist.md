## C. Mental Models
Guess prices, then install a system. Flywheel leftovers beat admin leftovers. Easy close = raise. One $10k > five small retainers. Pipeline is stability, not retainers. Baseline data is how you get the next price. Bleeding-edge (MCP) can be a sales lever — and a complexity trap. YouTube can be the front of the house once delivery is staffed.

## D. Procedures
1. Diagnose leak; prescribe outcome (not “chatbot”).
2. Simple tools; customize templates, do not sell the pack.
3. Hours × rate × 4 × 12 = annual story.
4. Package + high anchor; do not blurt a number.
5. If close >40–50% with no friction → raise (his rule).
6. Write inclusions + change-request.
7. Prototype; 1 week internal QA (sample + real + edges); 1 week client QA.
8. Collect baseline or you will not have a case study.
9. Multi-thread the account.

Questions: How do they do it by hand? Baseline hours? Would a retainer be five small fires? Signals: no send on outreach; >50% close. Qualify: leftover with numbers.
