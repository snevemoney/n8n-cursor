## C. Mental Models
Future-proof is the AI-native version of the job you have, not a new title. Relative expertise is enough to get named. Taste is residual human liability. Context is private intern-onboarding. Speed without a done-line is infinite polish. Jarvis is unattended vending machines plus rare slot-machines. Saying no to AI is the elite tell. Insurance is branched expertise, not five gigs and a resignation.

## D. Procedures
1. Become nameable: one allowed tool, one weekly work task, write before/after, show a human (permissioned).
2. Taste loop: study best → library → why → push corrections into the project.
3. Never start from a blank chat on recurring work — load the campaign/docs.
4. Before a build: metric + baseline + done. Stop at done.
5. Day audit: predictable triggers. For each: trigger without me? model needed? default script/workflow.
6. If stacking: one branch, contract check, do not burn FT. Public or quiet — something that is yours.

Questions: How long before/after? What still needs judgment? Must I be the trigger? Do we need a model? What does done look like? Does my contract allow a side stream?

Signals: someone at work already calls you the AI person. Disqualify: shipping unreviewed text; agent-for-Stripe-number.
