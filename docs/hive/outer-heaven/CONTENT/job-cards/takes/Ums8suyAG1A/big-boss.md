## D. Procedures

1. **Write the goal** (outcome + done-when), not the click path.
2. **Onboard:** who we are, good/bad examples, never-list, week shape (`session-bootstrap`).
3. **One SOP** you will actually reuse (`interview-to-desk`). Not a tour of every plugin.
4. **Review the artifact.** Open it. Name gaps (CTR missing). Correct until you would use it.
5. **Then** connect an existing tool. Ask-before-write. Lead Hunter ≠ Gmail. Send/pay/deploy HITL.
6. **Score** vs last month (hours, leads). No job-loss % as FACT.
7. **Draft ≠ send.** A 50-row sheet with `needs review` is a list, not a campaign.

**Qualify / frame:** Path C / us. How to manage agents, not a Clay outreach SKU and not a jobs forecast.
