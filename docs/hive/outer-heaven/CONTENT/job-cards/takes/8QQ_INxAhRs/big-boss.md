## D. Procedures

1. **Pick a default chair** (ours: Cursor + Grok). Close parallel “custom project” tabs.
2. **Write the router.** Where do identity, wiki, skills, worlds, decisions live?
3. **Load context** (who / business / don’ts). Gut-check: teammate or stranger?
4. **List tier-one connections** (revenue, customers, calendar, comms, tasks, meetings). Prefer scoped API/CLI. Do not paste secrets.
5. **Add capabilities as thin skills.** Repeated prompt → skill. After each run: like / dislike / update.
6. **Work in phases.** One job per session. Clear. Hand the artifact to the next named desk.
7. **Verify before you trust.** Click, source, persona. Reject 70% “done.”
8. **Earn cadence.** Only battle-tested skills get a schedule. Owner named.
9. **Permission with keys.** If send/pay/publish is possible, remove the capability.
10. **On a miss:** update router + skill; demand the exact source. Not “try harder.”
11. **Team later:** teach after you can walk it; one shared read-only store; do not duplicate.
