Beats, in order:
- AI-centric selling doesn’t work. Selling agents/workflows as products is a crowded commoditized market.
- People offer template libraries you can “resell for $5,000 a month” while charging $200 for the bundle — race to the bottom.
- LinkedIn outreach bot example: flashiest bot pitched as “LinkedIn outreach agent” → nobody cares.
- Reframe: system that generates qualified leads without spending on ads → people see value.
- Shift: stop selling AI tools; sell outcomes. Spotlight the pain. Frame around time, money, and focus.
- What to sell: AI solutions. Workflow = tool. Solution = tool tied to an actual business pain point.
