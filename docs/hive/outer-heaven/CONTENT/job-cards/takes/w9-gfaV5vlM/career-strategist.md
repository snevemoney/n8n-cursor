## C. Mental Models
Neon ≠ new food. Lowest-view build can be the highest ROI. Doctor/partner, not template reseller. Taxi / pill / hammer analogies: destination, pain gone, remodel value. Scope is self-protection. Face on the Loom is the hire signal. Tech obsession is the beginner trap he also fell in.

## D. Procedures
1. Pick one niche in ~10 minutes: weekly repeat? pay fast? you already speak it?
2. Message 5–10: 15 min, quantify bottleneck, where AI does and does not help, no pitch unless asked.
3. On the call: Listen, Repeat the pattern, Poke (whose hours, $/h, errors, copy-paste, 9–noon fire, one weekly fire).
4. Rank pains with hours/cost/frequency/error.
5. 15 min map (trigger, steps, data, done) → 60 min rough build → 15 min face Loom (before / solution / result).
6. Do not multi-agent, five-vendor, or platform-build Tuesday’s bottleneck.
7. Math: current cost × % automated = savings; price a fraction; write in/out/timeline/duties/pay.
8. Early: free/cheap or guarantee for proof; collect before/after; then rerun with proof.

Questions: Where are you leaking time, money, or focus? Whose hours? One fire? Signals: “sell me an agent.” Qualify: repeat + can pay + you speak it.
