Framework every time: diagnose → solve → value → price. Template bundles don’t do that. Onboarding example: 5h/week → automate 80% → 200+ h/year × $50 = $10k → charge $3k. - Step 4: math the owner understands. 10h × $25 = $250/wk → $1k/mo → $12k/yr; 60% automated = $600/mo / $7k+ yr; charge $3k, payback ~5 months. Value ≠ your hours ($1,200 in 2h = $600/h — they bought the outcome). Scope: objective, in/out, timeline, client duties, payment. Underscope was his #1 early mistake.
