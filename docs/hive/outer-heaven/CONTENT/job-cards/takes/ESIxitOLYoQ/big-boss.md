## B. Atomic Knowledge

### HITL until a campaign has already won
- **Claim:** Skills stay interactive. Do not YOLO a list machine until you are duplicating a winning campaign.
- **Reasoning:** Skilled labor needs a human who can see where the skill went wrong. YOLO hides the break.
- **Mechanism:** Terminal back-and-forth: pull 50 → report fit → human confirms exclusions / employee cap / score threshold → then (maybe) YOLO to a final output.
- **Evidence:** He refuses YOLO at the start; later says “Enter Yolo mode until we have a final output we are happy with” only after exclusions are agreed.
- **Conditions:** New vertical or new ICP. Works when the operator can read a 50-row sample.
- **Exceptions:** Markets he already understands (software lists that always leak agencies) can carry stored conditionals.
- **Action:** First 50 + human confirm is the checkable stop. Not “dump 500 and send.”
- **Confidence:** high
