## D. Procedures
**Questions:** Where are you losing the most time in your business? What processes do you wish could run themselves?
**Signals:** A weekly-hours answer you can annualize.
**Qualify:** they can name a recurring time sink.
**Frame:** I’m saving you $X/year; I’m charging a fraction now.
**Objections:** “That’s a cost” → fraction-of-value; “show me the workflow” → after diagnosis.
**Avoid:** leading with the canvas; listing tools (hammer/nails/saws).
**When to change:** if they will not name time-loss, you do not have a no-brainer price.
