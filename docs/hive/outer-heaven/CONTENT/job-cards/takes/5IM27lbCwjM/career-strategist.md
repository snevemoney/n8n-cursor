## D. Procedures
1. Ship a similar build on a public channel (for-fun is allowed).
2. Leave a reachable email in the description.
3. When a stranger emails “I want something similar,” take calls.
4. Scope the build on the call.
5. Say whether you can do it.
6. Stop if they do not say yes — he does not describe chase.
