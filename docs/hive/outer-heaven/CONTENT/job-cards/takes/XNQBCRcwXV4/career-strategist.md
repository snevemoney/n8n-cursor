## C. Mental Models
Same model ≠ same results if context/taste differ — and also if you over-constrain. Managing agents ≈ managing people. Understanding stays human. Verification is the scarce skill, not prompt poetry.

## D. Procedures

Goals: harder-than-comfortable + verify methods + “not a prototype.”
