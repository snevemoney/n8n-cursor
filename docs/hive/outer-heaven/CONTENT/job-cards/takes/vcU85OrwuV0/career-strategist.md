## D. Procedures
1. Give why and who it is for (not “write an email about the delay”).
2. Negative-prompt: what not to do; intern line: assess and stop; no send/edit/delete until go.
3. When you have enough, act — do not “research everything and make a full plan” as the default.
4. Match effort; reach for Fable ~5–15%.
5. Prove-it: point to evidence; if unverified, say so; bake into skills/system files.
6. Do not ask Fable to show reasoning.
7. Say less; lead with outcome; pause only when the work needs you.
8. If the answer feels dumber/cheaper, consider a silent Opus route.

Questions: Why this task? What must it not do? What proves done? Signals: promo-limit, silent demote. Qualify: is this the 5–15%?
