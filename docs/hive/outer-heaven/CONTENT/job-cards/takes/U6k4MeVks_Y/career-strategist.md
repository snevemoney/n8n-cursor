2. Microphone: what you want, who it is for, CTA, “ask what I’m not thinking about.”
3. When you do not understand an option, ask for a breakdown; do not smash recommended.
4. Accept plan + auto. Watch context; at ~30% decide clear vs compact vs new chat in-folder.
5. If output is generic, add a skill + a screenshot; ask for three versions; pick one.
6. For an outside app, pick connector or plugin or CLI — one. Do not deploy without HITL.
7. Long jobs: write objective + done-when + log. Cap sub-agents / cheaper models if you ever touch ultra (we operate Cursor + Grok).
8. For work you cannot grade, get a second reviewer (human first).
9. Repeated work → skill (in our stack). Optional: raw / wiki / output + index.

Questions: What am I not thinking about? What is a tech stack here? Do I need this session or a new one? Which of three versions?

Signals: generic first pass (missing context); context >30%; overlapping skills; self-grade that only praises.
