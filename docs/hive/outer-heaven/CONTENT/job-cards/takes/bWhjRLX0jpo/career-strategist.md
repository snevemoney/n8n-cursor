## C. Mental Models
Early-stage is messy clocks, merch, and almost-leaked keys. Warm inbound beats cold. Roles follow energy (shy founders → editor on camera). Equity talk shows up at dinner: money in or accept dilution. Discount windows are real (2-year startup deal). Copyright/platform limits are a spoken value even when someone jokes about a second email. Progress = walked the whole path once, even with bad data.

## D. Procedures
1. If a job fires on a clock, rehearse with more than five minutes of margin.
2. When a notable name is on the waitlist: personal thanks, then homework (podcasts), then a meeting ask.
3. Pitch the product in rooms you already inhabit; let a “I want to prove myself” become a trial (library sessions).
4. If the bottleneck is growth not code, move the person.
5. Before filming or screensharing: hide API keys and “fancy code.”
6. After a test: list what worked vs what is wrong (data, logs, timing) before dinner.
7. Face-ID / auth paths: deliberately fail then continue — they caught a bug that way.
8. Money-in vs dilution: say it; do not force it.

Questions: Did the clock job have a rehearsal? Who on the list is already warm? What worked vs broken? Signals: waitlist celebrity; missing logs. Qualify: opted-in name, not a scrape.
