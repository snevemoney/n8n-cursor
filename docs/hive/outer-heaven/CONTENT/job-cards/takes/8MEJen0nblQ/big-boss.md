## D. Procedures

1. **Write the goal** (Hormozi). If you cannot, stop collecting offers and tools.
2. **Name the who** as tightly as HVAC-in-Seattle. If you cannot, you are in niche paralysis — do not hunt.
3. **Study that who** until you can say what success is for them. Love the customer before the tactic.
4. **Find the clog/leak** in their words (phones, dispatch, data leaving). Not “need AI.”
5. **Write the story** as an outcome they can be accountable for (“I pick up the phone”; “data lives here”).
6. **Make a proof object** they can point at (server-on-the-table shape — our analog is a walkthrough / live proof, not a cert PDF).
7. **Verify intent** on anything you built: correct, sustainable, secure. Do not hand-wave vibe code.
8. **Tool filter:** does this take weight off *this* goal? If no, skip the viral release.
9. **Out of domain:** read AI-natives + domain experts; tool-call or build inputs; IP only if Evens wants it.
10. **Team enablement (learn only):** one catalyst job — big, soon, scary, wanted, impossible the old way. Cheap model for grunt, expensive for the call. Do not cut people off mid-month as a morality play.
11. **Comms:** listen to the monster story; answer with a specific useful story. No staff-replacement pitch.
12. **Close watch:** Substack/Skool/ClickUp code are *their* magnets, not ours.

**Qualify / frame:** Podcast for AI-agency sellers + operators. HVAC/legal are examples. ICP `us` only. Live hunt parked.
**Objections:** “Pick HVAC Seattle” — example, not a new id; `local-pro` already exists and stays parked. “Join his Slack” — community close. “Sell AI packages” — he said that is why you are not selling.
**Avoid:** Billboard/ads/drop-by/free-work as a desk action; install Claude/Codex; quote 76% / $200M / token bars as FACT; new hunt; unpark Normand.
