## D. Procedures
1. Write the outcome in one sentence a GM can repeat (pickup, not "AI").
2. Name a demographic you can love in a city.
3. Find the room they already occupy (not a LinkedIn agent pile).
4. Bring a holdable proof (where the data lives / who answers).
5. If enabling a team: one scary close project, not a credit bucket.
6. Route cheap models first.
