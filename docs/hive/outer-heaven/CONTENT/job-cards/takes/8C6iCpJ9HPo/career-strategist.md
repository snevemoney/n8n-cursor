## C. Mental Models
Employment can coexist with public learning. Authenticity > expert banner. Views are not the KPI; interested humans are. Price is value, not hours or node-count. People do not care how you fixed it. Time / money / focus are the three pay-me doors. Doctor > pharmacist. Prototype failure is progress. SME humility is a trust signal. Robustness = watched breaks + guardrails. You can monetize without founding a company.

## D. Procedures
1. Keep the day job. Build in public for learning. Put a HITL email in the description. No hard CTA required.
2. When someone asks to talk: take the call; talk value; you may throw a price (he did after almost working free).
3. To price: map manual process, hours, $ per hour, intangibles. Aim to show ≥2 of: time saved, money made/saved, quality/consistency.
4. From zero: share the journey → diagnose their leftover → pick any tool that solves it → prototype that can fail → name that you need their SME → watch live inputs → guardrail patterns → then price.
5. Do not sell “agent.” Sell the leftover job (here: morning ideas for LinkedIn nurture).

Questions: How do you do this by hand? How many hours? What is an hour worth? Which two of three ROI legs move? Signals: inbound that wants a friend/call, not a procurement packet. Qualify: real leftover, not a buzzword.
