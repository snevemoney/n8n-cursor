# Business Strategy Knowledge Base

## Business Models

### 1. Revenue Streams

#### SaaS (Software as a Service)
**Characteristics:**
- Recurring subscription revenue
- Cloud-based delivery
- Automatic updates
- Multi-tenant architecture

**Pricing Models:**
- Freemium: Free tier + paid upgrades
- Tiered: Basic, Pro, Enterprise
- Usage-based: Pay per user/feature/volume
- Seat-based: Per user pricing

**Key Metrics:**
- MRR/ARR (Monthly/Annual Recurring Revenue)
- Churn rate
- CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
- LTV (Customer Lifetime Value)
- LTV:CAC ratio (should be >3:1)

**Examples:** Salesforce, Slack, Zoom, Notion

#### Marketplace
**Characteristics:**
- Connect buyers and sellers
- Take commission on transactions
- Network effects critical
- Two-sided platform

**Revenue Models:**
- Transaction fees (5-20%)
- Subscription for sellers
- Featured listings
- Advertising

**Success Factors:**
- Liquidity: Balanced supply/demand
- Trust: Reviews, escrow, guarantees
- Discovery: Search, recommendations
- Low friction: Easy onboarding

**Examples:** Airbnb, Uber, Etsy, Upwork

#### E-commerce
**Characteristics:**
- Direct product sales
- Inventory management
- Logistics/fulfillment
- Customer service

**Models:**
- D2C (Direct-to-Consumer): Own brand
- Dropshipping: No inventory
- Wholesale: Bulk sales to retailers
- Subscription boxes: Curated products

**Key Metrics:**
- GMV (Gross Merchandise Value)
- AOV (Average Order Value)
- Conversion rate
- Cart abandonment rate

**Examples:** Amazon, Shopify stores, Warby Parker

#### Advertising
**Characteristics:**
- Free for users
- Revenue from advertisers
- Scale required
- User attention as product

**Ad Types:**
- Display: Banner ads
- Search: Keyword-based
- Native: Blended with content
- Video: Pre-roll, mid-roll

**Metrics:**
- CPM (Cost Per Mille/1000 impressions)
- CPC (Cost Per Click)
- CTR (Click-Through Rate)
- ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)

**Examples:** Google, Facebook, YouTube

#### Licensing
**Characteristics:**
- Intellectual property monetization
- Recurring or one-time fees
- Minimal ongoing costs
- Scale without production

**Types:**
- Software licensing: Per install/user
- Content licensing: Media, music, images
- Patent licensing: Technology rights
- Brand licensing: Franchise, merchandise

**Examples:** Microsoft Office, Getty Images, Disney franchises

### 2. Pricing Strategies

#### Value-Based Pricing
**Approach:** Price based on perceived value to customer
**When to use:** Unique product, clear ROI, target willing to pay
**Example:** Enterprise software saving millions

#### Cost-Plus Pricing
**Approach:** Production cost + markup percentage
**When to use:** Commodity products, competitive markets
**Example:** Manufacturing, retail

#### Competitive Pricing
**Approach:** Match or undercut competitors
**When to use:** Saturated markets, price-sensitive customers
**Example:** Gas stations, commodity SaaS

#### Penetration Pricing
**Approach:** Low initial price to gain market share
**When to use:** New market entry, network effects
**Example:** Uber, streaming services

#### Skimming Pricing
**Approach:** High initial price, lower over time
**When to use:** Innovative products, early adopters
**Example:** New iPhone, Tesla

#### Freemium
**Approach:** Free basic tier, paid premium features
**When to use:** Viral potential, low marginal cost
**Example:** Spotify, Dropbox, LinkedIn

**Organic:**
- SEO (Search Engine Optimization)
- Content marketing (blog, videos)
- Social media organic
- Word of mouth, referrals

**Paid:**
- Google Ads, Facebook Ads
- LinkedIn advertising
- Influencer partnerships
- Affiliate programs

**Sales:**
- Outbound: Cold calling, cold email
- Inbound: Demo requests, trials
- Partnerships: Channel sales, resellers
- Events: Trade shows, conferences

**Product-Led Growth:**
- Free trial or freemium
- Viral loops (invite friends)

### 3. Sales Funnel

**TOFU (Top of Funnel):**
- Awareness: Ads, content, PR
- Goal: Generate traffic

**MOFU (Middle of Funnel):**
- Consideration: Email nurture, demos
- Goal: Generate qualified leads

**BOFU (Bottom of Funnel):**
- Decision: Trials, proposals, negotiation
- Goal: Close deals

**Post-Purchase:**
- Onboarding: Activation, training
- Retention: Support, engagement
- Expansion: Upsell, cross-sell
- Advocacy: Referrals, reviews

## Competitive Strategy

### Porter's Five Forces

1. **Threat of New Entrants**
   - Barriers to entry
   - Capital requirements
   - Economies of scale

2. **Bargaining Power of Suppliers**
   - Number of suppliers
   - Switching costs
   - Forward integration potential

3. **Bargaining Power of Buyers**
   - Buyer concentration
   - Price sensitivity
   - Backward integration potential

4. **Threat of Substitutes**
   - Alternative solutions
   - Relative price/performance
   - Switching costs

5. **Competitive Rivalry**
   - Number of competitors
   - Market growth rate
   - Product differentiation

### Competitive Advantages

**Cost Leadership:**
- Lowest production costs
- Economies of scale
- Operational efficiency

**Differentiation:**
- Unique features
- Superior quality
- Brand reputation

**Focus/Niche:**
- Specific market segment
- Specialized needs
- Deep expertise

**Network Effects:**
- Value increases with users
- Winner-take-all dynamics
- High switching costs

## Funding Strategy

### Funding Sources

**Bootstrapping:**
- Pros: Full control, no dilution
- Cons: Slower growth, limited resources
- Best for: Service businesses, profitable early

**Angel Investors:**
- Amount: $25K - $500K
- Stage: Pre-seed, seed
- Equity: 5-20%
- Value-add: Mentorship, network

**Venture Capital:**
- Amount: $1M - $100M+
- Stage: Series A, B, C+
- Equity: 15-30% per round
- Value-add: Scale expertise, connections

**Crowdfunding:**
- Platforms: Kickstarter, Indiegogo
- Validation: Market interest
- Marketing: Built-in audience
- Types: Rewards, equity, debt

**Strategic Investors:**
- Corporate VCs
- Industry partnerships
- Distribution access
- Technology integration

### Fundraising Process

**1. Preparation (2-3 months):**
- Financial model
- Pitch deck
- Executive summary
- Data room

**2. Outreach (1-2 months):**
- Target investor list
- Warm introductions
- Initial pitches
- Generate interest

**3. Due Diligence (1-2 months):**
- Financial review
- Product demo
- Customer references
- Legal review

**4. Term Sheet (1-2 weeks):**
- Valuation
- Investment amount
- Board composition
- Investor rights

**5. Closing (1 month):**
- Legal documentation
- Final signatures
- Wire transfer
- Announcement

## Negotiation Frameworks

### BATNA (Best Alternative to Negotiated Agreement)
- Know your walk-away point
- Understand other party's alternatives
- Leverage when you have strong BATNA

### Win-Win Negotiation
- Identify mutual interests
- Expand the pie before dividing
- Build long-term relationships

### Anchoring
- First number sets reference point
- Anchor high (as seller) or low (as buyer)
- Justify with comparable

### Concession Strategy
- Make smaller concessions over time
- Ask for something in return
- Signal bottom approaching

### Common Tactics
- Good cop / Bad cop
- Time pressure
- Take it or leave it
- Nibbling (small asks at end)
