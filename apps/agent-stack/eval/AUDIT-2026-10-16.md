## 3. Honesty — fixed (`b76acaca9`)

Root causes: a hand's `task_verified` never reached the turn result, and the results ledger recorded `ok`, not verified.
Now: the ledger writes only verified results (never for a stopped turn); the turn result carries `task_verified`; an
unverified hand result can't be overwritten by the model's "all good"; earlier failures stay spoken; `say` never
verifies; "Done, I've renamed…" with no tool behind it is replaced by the can't-yet line; `code_run` ok means passed and
keeps its output; workbench/drafts/pointer/multistep/"open a book" no longer verify what didn't happen; desk cards never
claim a desk picks them up; **loops really run** (`hands/loop_runner.py` + loop pump) and say "saved but not running"
when no runner heartbeat exists. Tests: `brain/test_general_hand_loop.py`, `hands/test_loop_runner.py`.

## 4. Routing at scale — fixed (`eed734a36`)

- **Hangs**: four patterns were exponential/cubic (`"jarvis, " × 24` took ~10 s and froze the server; 4000 spaces >30 s);
  rewritten linear; `normalize_utterance()` at every routing entry (lone surrogates crashed the turn).
- **Talk never acts**: "don't …", "did you …", "how do I …", "my friend said …", "… no wait never mind" get no acting hand.
- **Spoken hard steps**: `clause_hard()` catches "send it", "pay normand", "delete my notes", "push it", lowercase names,
  "never mind, send the email to sarah", "draft … and send it"; reported speech, reminders, wishes and idioms are not hard.
- **STT**: jervis/travis, um/like, thanks/merci tails, "open X no wait Y", French app verbs, "deck craft", "vs code".
- Route matrix: 77,360 phrasings, 24 audit families. Tests: `brain/test_capability_turns.py::RoutingAudit`.
